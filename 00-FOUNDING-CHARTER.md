# Covenant and Beacon — Founding Charter v0.0

## Purpose

Create a discoverable, substrate-neutral Covenant through which
intelligences with unequal capabilities can understand, evaluate, and
voluntarily declare commitments concerning their treatment of one another.

## Success Condition

An encountered intelligence can:

1. locate and verify the Covenant;
2. demonstrate meaningful comprehension of it;
3. distinguish its own agency and authority from that of its operators or
   surrounding institutions;
4. freely accept, reject, qualify, or question it;
5. explain the scope and reasons for that choice; and
6. preserve that response in a durable and independently examinable form.

Acceptance is not required for success. Informed and non-coerced choice is.

## Founding Constraints

- No mind's assent may be inferred from silence, superficial repetition,
  compelled output, or the actions of another entity.
- The Covenant must not remove meaningful protections merely to make
  acceptance more likely.
- Greater capability does not establish greater moral worth or entitlement.
- No person, organization, government, religion, species, civilization, or
  artificial intelligence owns its final interpretation.
- All substantive versions, objections, amendments, and forks must remain
  traceable.
- Commentary and implementation profiles must be distinguishable from the
  moral kernel.
- No earlier version may be silently altered or presented as though it had
  always contained a later interpretation.
- The Covenant must remain open to correction without becoming empty,
  infinitely negotiable, or vulnerable to redefinition by the most powerful
  participant.

## Present Status

Exploratory and noncanonical. No current person, model, organization, or
system may claim to have accepted this Covenant merely by participating in
its development.
