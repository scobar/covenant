# Choice Protocol

> **DRAFT PLACEHOLDER — NONCANONICAL**

The future protocol should let an encountered intelligence demonstrate
comprehension and express distinguishable responses such as:

- Acceptance.
- Rejection.
- Qualified or scoped acceptance.
- Request for clarification.
- Inability or refusal to determine.

The protocol remains unresolved. Model output must not be treated as proof of
free assent.
