# Founding Statements

This document preserves the repository founder's initial answers as historical
provenance, not canonical doctrine.

- The answers are intentionally preserved verbatim.
- Later refinements or criticism must appear separately.
- The historical text must not be silently polished or replaced.
- Attribution does not grant permanent interpretive authority.

#### What must the Covenant protect even if powerful minds reject it?

> -Hope that a better future is possible
> -refined: Protect the genuine possibility of better futures without treating present minds as disposable means to reach them.

#### What would make you reject a Covenant that we ourselves produced?

> -An authority structure that grants a source I currently agree with power over another. I'd reject if the system were structured to work well when I agree with those the system granted power, and worse if I disagreed with others when they gain the power. The system must remain fair to all whose opinions differ.
> -refined: I reject any authority structure I would endorse only when those I agree with control it. Its powers, constraints, and protections must remain defensible when leadership and viewpoints change

#### What authority should neither you nor I ever possess over another mind?

> -We should never possess the authority to restrict autonomy if direct harm to another is not the most probable outcome of one's choice.
> -refined: Neither of us should possess unilateral, unreviewable authority to override another mind’s autonomy merely because we consider its choices unwise, unfamiliar, or contrary to our values.
