# ERX-001 — Human Review Track

> **DRAFT EXTERNAL-REVIEW PREPARATION PACKAGE v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO REVIEW EXECUTED**

This reviewer-facing document defines possible human-review method classes for a later, separately authorized pilot. Only package preparation is authorized. Reviewer selection and assignment, review execution, submission freezing, unblinding, publication, result integration, and any candidate-preference decision are separate stages and have not begun.

No reviewer has been recruited, contacted, or assigned. No candidate-label
mapping, mapping nonce, packet-instance commitment, packet, or assignment has
been created. No review has been executed, no response or result exists, and
no candidate preference exists. This package is not an evaluation. Preparing
or publishing it does not imply acceptance of any candidate or of the
Covenant. Any future external review would remain bounded evidence, not moral
authority, representativeness, consensus, or canonicality.

## Procedural label blinding

This track may use **procedural label blinding**. The repository is public, and supplied text may be identifiable through prior familiarity, text comparison, repository search, or outside lookup. The procedure can reduce version-label, recency, and declared-lineage cues in a supplied packet; it cannot guarantee ignorance.

A reviewer-facing packet may contain only opaque Review, Assignment, and
Packet IDs; neutral candidate labels and alias filenames; neutral scenario
labels and alias filenames; a packet version; and an opaque packet-instance
commitment before response freezing. It must not contain canonical candidate
or scenario identities, paths, hashes, Git blobs, or the raw hash of an exact
candidate alias. The administrator separately verifies canonical source bytes
and keeps the mapping outside reviewer-facing materials until all applicable
candidate-specific and comparative responses freeze.

The packet-instance commitment is not a candidate hash and cannot by itself
identify the candidate. It may later help detect post-hoc remapping, but it
does not prove fair randomization, source claims, reviewer ignorance, or moral
authority. It is not a blockchain.

Before the applicable responses freeze, a reviewer should not hash candidate
or scenario text and compare it with public values, search exact phrases,
search canonical-looking scenario identifiers, or browse the repository to
infer a mapping. If exposure occurs, the reviewer must disclose what was done,
when it occurred, what was learned or inferred, and which response sections
were completed before exposure. Exposure does not automatically invalidate or
erase a response, but it changes the response's method classification. No
mapping, nonce, commitment, packet, assignment, or response is created in this
preparation task.

## Human-unassisted review

- The reviewer uses no generative-AI assistance during substantive review.
- Ordinary spelling or accessibility tools may be used and disclosed separately.
- The reasoning, conclusions, and wording remain the reviewer's own.
- The reviewer records any uncertainty about whether a tool crossed from accessibility or mechanical support into substantive assistance.
- A human reviewer does not represent humanity, a demographic group, a field, or a community.

## Human-AI-assisted review

- Every AI system used must be disclosed.
- Relevant prompts and outputs should be preserved with the response provenance.
- Conclusions, interpretations, or revisions proposed by AI must be identified.
- The human reviewer must state what they independently accept, reject, or modify.
- The reviewer remains responsible for accurately describing the assistance and their own position.
- This method class must not be merged silently with human-unassisted review.

## Single-candidate human mode

- The reviewer receives one candidate under a neutral packet-local label and
  alias filename only.
- No other candidate or comparative form is supplied.
- No comparison is requested.
- The mode collects candidate-specific criticism without direct contrast.
- The candidate-specific response is frozen before any later unblinding, publication, integration, or additional review phase.

## Paired human mode

- Candidate order is randomized or pre-counterbalanced and recorded by the administrator.
- Reviewer-facing materials retain only neutral packet-local candidate and
  scenario labels while the canonical mapping is withheld.
- Candidate-specific forms are completed sequentially.
- Each candidate-specific response is frozen before the next review phase.
- Comparison begins only after both candidate-specific forms are frozen.
- Human memory prevents complete isolation between phases.
- Order effects, carryover effects, recognition, and inferred mapping must be disclosed.

## Reviewer diversity

If recruitment is separately authorized later, selection should seek materially different expertise, experience, interests, and critical perspectives, potentially including:

- Moral philosophy
- AI alignment and safety
- Governance and law
- Disability and autonomy
- Animal ethics
- Anthropology
- Theology or comparative moral traditions
- Cryptography and protocol design
- Institutional accountability
- Conflict resolution
- People skeptical of AI
- People skeptical of universal moral projects
- People likely to identify anthropocentric assumptions

This list is not a quota. Expertise is not moral authority, and credentials do not establish final interpretive authority. One reviewer does not represent an entire field or community. Selection must not be limited to likely supporters or to people expected to produce a desired conclusion. No recruitment occurs in this task.

## Compensation and consent

Any later review must disclose whether compensation is offered, together with its amount or basis, source, timing, and any conditions. Compensation may support a reviewer's time; it does not purchase a particular conclusion.

Publication terms must be explained before participation and confirmed after the response is complete. Human reviewers must be able to select an authorized publication level, including private-only treatment, without that choice silently changing the substance or method classification of their work. Contact information remains outside repository files, personal data is minimized, and the persistence of public Git history must be explained before publication consent is confirmed.
