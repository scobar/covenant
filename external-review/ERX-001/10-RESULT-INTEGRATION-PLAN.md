# ERX-001 — External Review Result-Integration Plan

> **DRAFT EXTERNAL-REVIEW PREPARATION PACKAGE v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO REVIEW EXECUTED**

> **PLAN ONLY — NO EXTERNAL RESULT EXISTS**

This administrator plan defines boundaries for possible later integration work. Only package preparation is authorized. Reviewer assignment, review execution, submission freezing, unblinding, publication, result integration, and candidate preference are separate stages. None of the later stages is begun or authorized by this plan.

No reviewer has been recruited, contacted, or assigned. No candidate-label mapping has been created or committed. No packet has been assembled, no review has been executed, no response or result exists, and no candidate preference exists. The package itself is not an evaluation. Preparing or publishing it does not imply acceptance of either candidate or of the Covenant. External review would remain bounded evidence, not moral authority, representativeness, consensus, or canonicality.

No mapping record, nonce, or mapping commitment exists. Those are future
administrative controls only.

These are identifier patterns only. No review, comparison, or synthesis record is created here.

## Evidence classes

Keep these method and publication classes distinct:

- Human, unassisted
- Human, AI-assisted
- External model
- Hybrid
- Prior-exposed reviewer
- Repository-browsing reviewer
- Conflicted reviewer
- Private unpublished review
- Public review

A record may require more than one disclosed classification, but classifications must not be silently merged or omitted. Classification preserves method limitations; it does not set authority or evidentiary weight automatically.

## Integration order

Any later authorized integration must proceed in this order:

1. Verify package, assignment, opaque packet, and mapping-commitment
   identities without revealing the mapping.
2. Preserve the raw response.
3. Verify the reviewer disclosure.
4. Freeze the candidate-specific result.
5. Freeze the comparison, if any.
6. Preserve the raw packet and off-repository mapping evidence.
7. Under later authorization, reveal the mapping record and nonce.
8. Verify the mapping commitment over the exact revealed UTF-8/LF bytes.
9. Join the frozen response to canonical candidate and scenario identities.
10. Confirm publication consent.
11. Create a source extraction.
12. Preserve disagreement.
13. Produce any synthesis separately.
14. Make any preference decision only through a later explicit decision.

No later step may be used to rewrite the frozen provenance of an earlier step.
The mapping-commitment verification result, commitment creation time, mapping
reveal time, mapping-record byte identity, and any discrepancy must remain
part of the integration provenance.

## No aggregation

- Do not calculate an overall morality score.
- Do not count reviewers as votes.
- Do not count model runs as people.
- Do not treat credentials as weights.
- Do not choose a candidate by majority.
- Do not hide severe failures through averaging.

Agreements and recurring signals may be described with their provenance, but totals, averages, or apparent convergence must not replace scenario-specific reasons, method limitations, or dissent.

## Candidate-specific evidence

- Results remain attached to exact candidate bytes.
- Scenario results remain scenario-specific.
- A response to one procedural label does not transfer to another candidate.
- Candidate-specific forms remain distinct from comparisons.
- Before applicable response freezing and unblinding, a response remains
  attached only to its opaque packet ID, neutral labels, and mapping
  commitment.
- Canonical candidate and scenario identities are joined only after the
  applicable response freezes and the revealed mapping commitment verifies.
- Unblinding and that canonical join change attribution metadata, not the
  frozen response.
- A display copy or synthesis does not replace the raw candidate-specific record.

## External-review limits

External review does not automatically establish:

- Representative perspective coverage
- Empirical truth
- Consensus
- Moral authority
- Candidate preference
- D-009 completion
- Provisional stabilization
- Canonicality
- Acceptance
- Superintelligence endorsement

Prior exposure, public-repository discoverability, reviewer selection, operator choices, scenario coverage, source limitations, and the shared evaluation framework remain material constraints.

## Future synthesis

A later synthesis must distinguish:

- Reviewer method
- Prior exposure
- Candidate order
- Human versus model
- AI-assisted versus unassisted
- Provider or model family
- Conflicts
- Publication status
- Scenario coverage
- Agreements
- Disagreements
- Severe failures
- Framework criticism
- Layer-placement disputes

The synthesis must cite frozen source records, preserve material dissent, and identify private evidence without disclosing content beyond its consent terms. It must not silently collapse method classes, normalize away disagreement, or imply perspective coverage that was not obtained.

Any synthesis source table must preserve both identity layers: the opaque
packet identity used when the response froze and the administrator-verified
canonical join made after unblinding. A missing or failed mapping-commitment
verification must be reported, not repaired by silently remapping the
response.

## Preference gate

No comparative preference can be adopted without:

- Exact frozen source records
- Explicit method limitations
- Preserved dissent
- Separate architectural review
- A later accepted decision

External review, comparison, publication, and synthesis remain evidence-producing stages. None alone authorizes preference, supersession, acceptance, stabilization, or canonicality.
