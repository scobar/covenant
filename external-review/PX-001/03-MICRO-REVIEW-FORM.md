## Plan-Only Status and Limits

PX-001 defines an optional process micro-pilot only. It may remain dormant
indefinitely without blocking the project. It does not require a human
volunteer to exist or an external model to be available, and failure to obtain
either optional unit is not a project failure. It creates no entitlement to
anyone's unpaid labor and no obligation for Scott to spend money.

This blank form does not select or contact anyone; select or run a model;
assemble a packet; create a mapping, nonce, or commitment; execute or publish a
review; or create candidate preference. A later accepted decision is required
before any invitation, model selection, packet assembly, or execution. Any
later external review would remain bounded criticism—not moral authority,
representativeness, consensus, canonicality, or superintelligence assent.

The form is planned for at most one uncompensated volunteer human-unassisted
paired micro-review and at most one zero-incremental-cost external-model paired
micro-review. The two possible units are independently optional. Neither has a
replacement requirement or completion minimum. A completed unit would be one
bounded process datapoint, not a pilot-success verdict or
candidate-preference signal.

The planned review uses exactly one frozen scenario, shown here only as
`Scenario 1`. Complete Candidate A and Candidate B separately, in that order,
before comparison. The form intentionally tests only eight targeted
dimensions. This subset was selected to limit unpaid burden and test the
central `Scenario 1` process. It is not the complete fifteen-dimension ERX-001
review or a complete framework evaluation, omitted dimensions are not treated
as passed, and the form does not satisfy the project's open minimum-coverage
decision. It does not count as complete perspective or clause coverage under
that open decision.
