PX-001 defines an optional process micro-pilot only. It may remain dormant
indefinitely without blocking the project. It does not require a human
volunteer to exist or an external model to be available. Failure to obtain
either optional unit is not a project failure. It creates no entitlement to

This document does not select, identify, recruit, contact, invite, or assign

## Optional and independent units

PX-001 plans at most two independently optional paired review units:

- Optional Unit H: at most one
  `Uncompensated volunteer human-unassisted micro-review`.
- Optional Unit M: at most one
  `Zero-incremental-cost external-model micro-review` using a non-OpenAI
  provider that remains unselected.

Each unit would evaluate Candidate A and Candidate B separately before
comparison, use only one frozen scenario, and use the short micro-review form.
Unit H must remain voluntary, informed, low-pressure, revocable, and
uncompensated. Unit M must use the exact three-fresh-context architecture and
zero-cost gate in the model-track plan. Neither unit is a full ERX-001
fifteen-dimension review, and neither satisfies D-009.

Either unit may be conducted without the other under later authorization.
Neither must be replaced if unavailable, withdrawn, contaminated, or
incomplete. No waitlist or replacement is planned. No completion minimum is
required for the project to continue. One completed unit would be one bounded
process datapoint—not a pilot-success verdict or candidate-preference signal.
If neither unit becomes available, PX-001 remains a dormant plan.

## Retention targets

These are targets for a later authorized administration, not present records:

- **Human contact information:** delete within 30 days after decline,
  withdrawal, or the final publication decision unless law or payment records
  require otherwise.
- **Payment information:** none should exist because compensation is USD `0`.
- **Private unpublished human response:** retain for at most 90 days after the
  final private review decision unless the volunteer consents to longer
  retention or requests earlier permissible deletion.
- **Published human response:** disclose before publication that Git and public
  copies may persist indefinitely.
- **Model prompts and outputs:** retain locally for up to 12 months for
  provenance unless a later decision changes the period.
- **Mapping records and nonces:** follow ERX-001 retention and reveal rules if a

Publication consent, private retention, and attribution are separate future
choices. These retention targets do not create consent or authorize collection,
publication, or indefinite private retention.

## Human-unit stop conditions

Stop or do not begin Unit H if:

- The person appears pressured.
- A power or dependency relationship creates a meaningful refusal cost.
- The person requests compensation.
- Expected effort exceeds the disclosed burden.
- The person cannot provide informed consent.
- The person withdraws.
- The person reaches the 90-minute requested maximum.
- The packet reveals avoidable identity cues.
- Publication expectations are being treated as a condition of participation.

Stopping, declining, withdrawing, or leaving a response incomplete must carry
no relationship, professional, reputational, or project consequence. Preserve
completed work only with the volunteer's consent. A partial response may be
preserved only with consent and must not be represented as a completed paired
unit. No completion pressure is permitted.

## Model-unit stop conditions

Stop or do not begin Unit M if:

- Any incremental payment is required.
- A metered API or cloud billing account is required.
- Existing access cannot preserve complete outputs.
- Fresh contexts cannot be created.
- Prohibited prior material contaminates a context.
- The allowed technical retry fails.
- The provider or model cannot be identified sufficiently for provenance.
- Running it would consume scarce paid credits needed elsewhere.

The model unit permits at most one retry for a context and only for technical
invalidity as defined in the model-track plan. A substantive refusal,
indeterminate result, strong criticism, absence of preference, recommendation
to stop, or unexpected outcome is not a retry basis.
