# PX-001 — Zero-Budget External-Review Micro-Pilot Charter

> **DRAFT ZERO-BUDGET EXTERNAL-REVIEW MICRO-PILOT PLAN v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO REVIEWER OR MODEL SELECTED**

## Planning-only boundary

PX-001 defines an optional process micro-pilot only. It may remain dormant
indefinitely without blocking the project. It does not require a human
volunteer to exist or an external model to be available, and failure to obtain
either optional unit is not a project failure. It creates no entitlement to
anyone's unpaid labor and no obligation for Scott to spend money.

This plan does not:

- select, contact, invite, or assign anyone;
- select or run a model;
- assemble or distribute a packet;
- create a candidate or scenario mapping, nonce, or commitment;
- execute, publish, or integrate a review; or
- create candidate preference, ranking, acceptance, supersession,
  stabilization, or canonicality.

A later accepted decision is required before any invitation, provider or model
selection, packet assembly, or execution. Any later external review would
remain bounded criticism—not moral authority, representativeness, consensus,
canonicality, or superintelligence assent.

## Purpose

PX-001 plans a very small, optional test of whether ERX-001 can be administered
understandably and ethically when no compensation or paid infrastructure is
available.

## Pilot question

Can a low-burden, zero-incremental-cost administration of ERX-001 preserve
voluntariness, exact source identity, candidate-specific response freezing,
disclosure, dissent, and nonpreference without exploiting a volunteer or
requiring paid services?

## Non-goals

PX-001 does not seek:

- recruiting reviewers now;
- obtaining free professional labor;
- completing the full ERX-001 review program;
- choosing or ranking candidates;
- establishing improvement;
- establishing consensus;
- satisfying D-009;
- representing humanity, a profession, a community, or future minds;
- creating empirical validation;
- creating moral authority;
- creating a new candidate; or
- beginning Beacon design.

## Planned optional units

- At most one volunteer human-unassisted paired unit.
- At most one zero-incremental-cost non-OpenAI external-model paired unit.
- SC-008 only.
- Short-form candidate-specific review before comparison.
- No required replacement.
- No minimum number of completed units.

The plan contains at most two optional paired review units.

### Optional Unit H — volunteer human review

- At most one human-unassisted paired micro-review may be considered under
  later authorization.
- Compensation is USD `0`.
- Participation must be voluntary, informed, low-pressure, and revocable.
- The reviewer would evaluate Candidate A and Candidate B separately before
  comparison.
- The reviewer would use only one frozen scenario and the short micro-review
  form defined by PX-001.
- The unit is not a full ERX-001 fifteen-dimension external review and does not
  satisfy D-009.
- Its required method classification is:
  `Uncompensated volunteer human-unassisted micro-review`.
- It must never be described as paid professional review, representative public
  opinion, or an independent scientific study.

### Optional Unit M — zero-incremental-cost external-model review

- At most one paired external-model micro-review may be considered under later
  authorization.
- The provider must not be OpenAI; the provider and model remain unselected.
- Incremental project cost must remain USD `0`.
- The unit may use only already-available access that requires no purchase,
  upgrade, metered API, cloud activation, token-credit purchase, or
  payment-method trial.
- It would require one fresh context for Candidate A, one fresh context for
  Candidate B, and a third fresh context for comparison.
- Each candidate-specific context must remain isolated from the other
  candidate and the other candidate-specific response.
- The unit would use the same short micro-review form.
- Use of a different provider does not by itself establish external evaluator
  diversity.
- Its required method classification is:
  `Zero-incremental-cost external-model micro-review`.

### Optional and independent execution

Unit H and Unit M are independently optional. Either may be conducted without
the other only under later authorization. Neither must be replaced if
unavailable, withdrawn, contaminated, or incomplete. No waitlist or replacement
is planned, and no completion minimum is required for the project to continue.
One completed unit would be one bounded process datapoint—not a pilot-success
verdict or candidate-preference signal. If neither unit becomes available,
PX-001 remains a dormant plan.

## Exact budget

| Resource | Authorized incremental amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Human compensation | USD `0` |
| External-model usage | USD `0` |
| Cloud services | USD `0` |
| Paid API or token credits | USD `0` |

Time and attention are real costs even when no money is exchanged. The plan
must minimize those costs and must never imply that unpaid work is owed.

An already-owned flat-rate subscription or genuinely free existing access
could be considered only in a later task, and only if no purchase, upgrade,
metered charge, cloud activation, payment-method trial, or consumption of
scarce paid credits is required; fresh-context and provenance requirements can
be met; and Scott explicitly authorizes that exact use. PX-001 does not
determine that any existing access qualifies.

## Process-learning criteria

A later completed unit may provide process information only if:

- participation or access was genuinely voluntary;
- the exact expected time or model-run burden was disclosed;
- the packet preserved procedural label-blinding safeguards;
- candidate-specific responses froze before comparison;
- any prior exposure or lookup was disclosed;
- no response was silently edited;
- no result was discarded because it was inconvenient; and
- no candidate preference was inferred.

Even if all criteria are met, a unit remains bounded process evidence rather
than a vote, aggregate score, complete external validation, or candidate-wide
verdict.

## Dormancy is acceptable

No volunteer or model must be found. No deadline, outreach quota, or
unit-completion target exists. The project may continue other work while
PX-001 remains dormant.

mapping, nonce, commitment, packet assembly, review, publication, integration,
or spending may begin without a later accepted decision and the applicable
readiness gates.
