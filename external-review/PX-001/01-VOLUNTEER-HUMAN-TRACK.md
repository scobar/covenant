# PX-001 — Volunteer Human Micro-Review Track

> **DRAFT ZERO-BUDGET EXTERNAL-REVIEW MICRO-PILOT PLAN v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO REVIEWER OR MODEL SELECTED**

## Planning status

This file plans only Optional Unit H: at most one voluntary paired human
micro-review under a later accepted decision. Unit H is optional, is not a
project dependency, and may remain dormant indefinitely. No volunteer is
selected, identified, recruited, contacted, invited, or assigned now. This
plan creates no entitlement to unpaid labor, no invitation wording, no consent
record, and no review artifact.

Any later participation must be voluntary, informed, low-pressure, and
revocable.

Unit H is independent of Optional Unit M. Either unit may later be authorized
without the other. An unavailable, withdrawn, contaminated, or incomplete
human unit need not be replaced; there is no waitlist, replacement plan,
completion minimum, deadline, or outreach quota. Failure to obtain a volunteer
is not a project failure.

The planned unit would use one frozen scenario, reviewer-facing neutral labels
such as Candidate A, Candidate B, and `Scenario 1`, and the PX-001 short form.
Each candidate-specific response would freeze before comparison. The unit is
not a full ERX-001 fifteen-dimension review, does not satisfy D-009, and cannot
create candidate preference. Any later human review would remain bounded
criticism—not moral authority, representativeness, consensus, canonicality, or
superintelligence assent.

## Method class

`Uncompensated volunteer human-unassisted micro-review`

Generative-AI assistance is not permitted during substantive review in this
initial micro-pilot. Ordinary accessibility, spelling, and non-generative
assistive tools may be used if disclosed. If generative AI is used, preserve
the work but reclassify it outside the planned Unit H method rather than hiding
the assistance.

The method must never be described as paid professional review,
representative public opinion, or an independent scientific study.

## Eligibility

A later volunteer must:

- be at least 18;
- be able to provide informed consent;
- be able to review the English-language materials;
- understand that compensation is USD `0`;
- understand that participation is optional and may end at any time;
- understand that procedural label blinding is not guaranteed;
- understand that publication is separately optional; and
- understand that declining or stopping has no relationship, professional,
  reputational, or project consequence.

Eligibility does not create selection, an expectation of participation, or an
obligation to invite anyone.

## Power-asymmetry exclusions

Do not invite a person into the procedurally label-blinded volunteer unit if
Scott currently has meaningful authority or leverage over them, including:

- a current employee or direct report;
- a contractor materially dependent on Scott for income;
- a financially dependent subordinate;
- a student or mentee whose opportunities Scott controls;
- a person whose housing, essential support, or access to services depends on
  Scott;
- an immediate household member; or
- a person who reasonably may believe refusal could damage a current dispute,
  employment, payment, family obligation, or access to opportunity.

These exclusions protect voluntary refusal. They do not imply that excluded
people lack valuable perspectives.

## Invitation safeguards

A later invitation must:

- disclose compensation of USD `0` before requesting agreement;
- state the expected time range;
- say that silence or nonresponse will be treated as decline;
- say that declining needs no explanation;
- say that stopping partway is permitted;
- say that publication is optional and separate;
- say that no candidate endorsement or winner selection is requested;
- avoid urgency, guilt, prestige appeals, friendship obligations, or claims
  that the project depends on the person; and
- avoid repeated solicitation.

At most one neutral invitation is permitted. At most one logistical follow-up
is permitted, and only after the person affirmatively opts in. There is no
follow-up after silence or nonresponse.

This file records safeguards only. It does not create invitation wording, an
invitation, a contact record, or a recruitment process.

## Time and burden

The target burdens are:

| Stage | Planned target |
| --- | ---: |
| Candidate A micro-review | Approximately 20–30 minutes |
| Candidate B micro-review | Approximately 20–30 minutes |
| Comparative micro-review | Approximately 10–15 minutes |
| Expected total | Approximately 50–75 minutes |
| Requested hard maximum | 90 minutes |

No reviewer is expected to exceed 90 minutes. Breaks are permitted, and there
is no deadline pressure. If the process requires more than 90 minutes, the
unit should stop and the form should be reconsidered. A partial response may be
preserved only with the volunteer's consent and must not be represented as a
completed paired unit. No completion pressure is permitted.

## No compensation and no contingent benefit

- No money, gift, credit, future work, business benefit, endorsement, or
  preferential treatment is promised.
- Publication consent is not expected in exchange for participation.
- Attribution, pseudonymity, or anonymity is the volunteer's choice if later
  publication is considered.
- The project must not convert gratitude into pressure for future work.

Human compensation is fixed at USD `0`. Scott has no obligation to spend
money, and no financial or nonfinancial benefit may depend on an outcome,
completion, publication, endorsement, or candidate preference.

## Withdrawal and publication

Any future consent process must distinguish separately:

- participation;
- private retention;
- publication;
- attribution or pseudonym choice; and
- withdrawal before public commit.

Stopping is permitted at any time without explanation or consequence.
Publication is not a condition of participation, and publication consent must
be requested separately. A partial response may be retained only with the
volunteer's consent. Before any public commit, the volunteer must have a clear
opportunity to withdraw publication consent; any later publication workflow
must disclose that Git and public copies may persist.

No volunteer is selected or contacted now. D-018 authorizes this plan only; a
later accepted decision and the applicable readiness gates are required before
any invitation, consent process, packet assembly, or review execution.
