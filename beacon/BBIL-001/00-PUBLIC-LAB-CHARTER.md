## Research question

> Can a rapid, same-tooling Codex subagent loop improve lower-level Beacon
> design while preserving explicit context honesty and keeping adaptive
> development evidence separate from sealed validation evidence?

BBIL-001 answers this question only by preparing a draft orchestration and
research architecture and by exercising that architecture with one bounded
synthetic capability probe. It creates no authority to generate, expose,
decode, classify, score, select, compare, or reveal a real Beacon candidate.

## Evidence and authority boundary

- Architecture prepared: `True`.
- Synthetic capability probe completed: `True`.
- Real Beacon candidate created or exposed: `False`.
- Empirical Beacon result created: `False`.
- Universal-decoding claim made: `False`.
- Covenant acceptance or commitment claim made: `False`.
- Provider-independent validation performed: `False`.
- SIG-002 authorized or created: `False`.

The synthetic probe tests orchestration capability only. Its same-tooling
result cannot establish clean holdout evidence, provider independence,
real-candidate readiness, treatment benefit, universal decodability, or final
interpretive authority.
