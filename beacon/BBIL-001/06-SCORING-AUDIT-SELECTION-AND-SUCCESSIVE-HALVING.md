## Frozen scoring hierarchy

- Use categorical Decoder Levels 0–4 only.
- Level 1 framing/hierarchy is the primary endpoint.
- Level 0 artificiality and Level 2 structured numeracy are nonregression
  guardrails.
- Levels 3 and 4 are exploratory.
- Levels 5–9 are absent.
- No aggregate, weighted, averaged, scalar, or winner score exists.

Each technically eligible response receives one fresh primary score and one
independent scratch-before-comparison audit. The auditor freezes its complete
categorical vector before reading the primary record. Exact agreement or
preserved dissent may freeze a score; an audit failure stops that candidate.
Same-tooling agreement is not external independent validation.

## Advancement gate

Reject a candidate after any:

- construction or equivalence failure;
- invalidity;
- contamination;
- Level 0 regression;
- Level 2 regression;
- parse ambiguity; or
- context leak.

Advance only an eligible candidate with the frozen Level 1 label
`Possible improvement`. If eligible candidates are indistinguishable,
preserve uncertainty rather than choosing a winner. Reviewer count, majority,
family order, favorable prose, hidden scalar, or narrative attractiveness may
not decide advancement.

## Adaptation cap

- Maximum adaptive generations: `2`.
- A third generation requires explicit later architectural review.
- No favorable-output selection.
- No scalar objective.
- No majority vote.
- No hidden winner score.

The synthetic capability probe uses a separate four-criterion pipeline test.
It is not a Beacon score and does not instantiate this pilot.
