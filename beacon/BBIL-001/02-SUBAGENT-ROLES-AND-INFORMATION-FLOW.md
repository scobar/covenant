## Ten separate roles

| Role | May receive | Must not receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Coordinator/custodian | Accepted authority, frozen architecture, role assignments, identifiers, commitments, custody metadata, and high-level frozen findings | Authority to reinterpret frozen evidence, select favorable results, or reveal sealed content outside a later authorization |
| 2. Candidate-design subagents | One frozen abstract semantic source, one declared manipulated variable, construction constraints, and permitted high-level frozen failure families | Raw sealed confirmation outputs, other active candidates, score records, mapping, favored outcome, or hidden winner information |
| 3. Equivalence and construction auditors | One candidate, the frozen source, invariants, parse requirements, and construction proof inputs | Decoder outputs, performance expectations, treatment preference, or selection outcome |
| 4. Blinding/packet custodian | Eligible candidate artifact, prompt-packet specification, allowlist, commitments, canaries, and opaque run identifier | Scoring judgments, expected decoder behavior, favored candidate, or authority to alter candidate bytes |
| 5. Blind decoder subagents | One candidate packet and the frozen neutral instruction only | Truth key, scorecard, expected outcome, other candidate, other decoder output, prior run, mapping, parent project context intentionally supplied, or follow-up |
| 6. Validity classifiers | One frozen decoder response, neutral instruction, run metadata, and context-honesty ledger | Truth key, scoring rubric, favored interpretation, selection state, or another candidate's output |
| 7. Primary scorers | One technically eligible frozen response, its authorized truth key, and its frozen categorical scorecard | Another candidate or condition, another score, mapping, expected winner, selector output, or adaptive preference |
| 8. Independent scratch-before-comparison auditors | The same authorized scoring inputs as the primary scorer, with the primary record withheld until the complete scratch result freezes | Primary result before scratch freeze, another candidate or condition, mapping, expected winner, or favorable-selection instruction |
| 9. Anonymous generation selector | Opaque eligible candidate IDs, frozen categorical Level 1 labels, Level 0/2 guardrails, validity, contamination, and equivalence dispositions | Candidate authorship, raw decoder prose where not required, treatment identity, hidden scalar score, majority signal, or expected winner |
| 10. Sealed-validation custodian | Frozen surface-novel design, sealed-validation protocol, provider-selection gate, commitments, and exposure records | Adaptive feedback after freeze, unapproved mapping disclosure, favorable retry authority, or permission to tune from validation outputs |

## Information-flow gates

```text
design -> equivalence/construction audit -> packet custody -> blind response
       -> validity classification -> primary score + independent scratch audit
       -> opaque generation selection

adaptive finding -> high-level frozen finding only -> later candidate design

frozen surface-novel design -> separate sealed-validation custody
```

Validity precedes scoring. Primary scoring and independent audit remain
separate until scratch freeze. Adaptive development and sealed validation do
not share raw feedback. No role receives final interpretive authority, and
reviewer multiplicity is not treated as a vote.
