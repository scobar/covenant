# BBIL-001 — Portable Blind-Iteration Workflow Skill

> **BBIL-001 v0.1 — DRAFT BLIND-ITERATION LAB ARCHITECTURE — SYNTHETIC CAPABILITY PROBE ONLY — NO REAL BEACON CANDIDATE, SCORE, HOLDOUT, OR RESULT**

> **Zero Beacon-specific context is intentionally supplied to the blind
> decoder; unavoidable system context, model-training priors, minimum interface
> instructions, hidden backend behavior, and any unproven workspace or
> provider isolation remain disclosed limitations.**

## Purpose and portability

This file is a portable workflow reference for orchestrating bounded,
context-honest candidate iteration with separate adaptive-development and
sealed-validation lanes. Its presence does not assert that the current Codex
client auto-discovers, automatically installs, or automatically enforces it.

Real-candidate use is explicitly prohibited until a later accepted decision
authorizes BBIL adaptive execution.

## Required inputs

- reviewed and accepted task authority;
- clean public checkpoint and protected-evidence baseline;
- frozen generation rules and abstract semantic source;
- declared single manipulated variable per candidate;
- equivalence, parse, reconstruction, and novelty requirements;
- frozen neutral instruction and context-honesty ledger;
- exposed control schema recorded without invention;
- frozen validity policy and categorical Levels 0–4 scorecard;
- canary and source-allowlist plan; and
- separate adaptive-development and sealed-validation custody plans.

## Preconditions

1. Verify repository identity, worktree, and real index.
2. Verify protected public evidence and decision boundaries.
3. Confirm that no private source access is required for the authorized task.
4. Confirm an explicit no-history control or record its absence.
5. Record repository, tool, and network controls as enforced, requested,
   unavailable, or unverified.
6. Stop if truth/scoring inputs cannot be separated from a blind decoder.
7. Confirm that real-candidate execution has separate accepted authority.

## Staged workflow

1. Freeze the context-honesty ledger, prompt packet, source allowlist, and
   canary commitments.
2. Derive isolated one-variable candidates from the shared abstract source.
3. Run construction, equivalence, unique-parse, reconstruction, and novelty
   audits.
4. Reject every candidate that fails a hard gate before decoder exposure.
5. Give each fresh blind decoder one eligible packet and the neutral
   instruction only; request one final response and no follow-up.
6. Freeze the exact response and metadata.
7. Run the mechanical canary and project-identifier scan.
8. Freeze a separate validity classification before scoring.
9. Run one fresh primary scorer and one independent scratch-before-comparison
   auditor per eligible response.
10. Apply Level 1 as primary, Levels 0/2 as guardrails, Levels 3/4 as
    exploratory, Levels 5–9 as absent, and no aggregate.
11. Select anonymously using only frozen hard-gate inputs and the
    `Possible improvement` label; preserve indistinguishability.
12. Stop after at most two adaptive generations pending later review.
13. Freeze a fresh surface realization and transfer it to separate sealed-
    validation custody without adaptive feedback.

## Roles and information boundaries

Use the ten roles in
[`02-SUBAGENT-ROLES-AND-INFORMATION-FLOW.md`](../02-SUBAGENT-ROLES-AND-INFORMATION-FLOW.md):
coordinator/custodian, candidate designers, equivalence/construction auditors,
packet custodian, blind decoders, validity classifiers, primary scorers,
independent auditors, anonymous selector, and sealed-validation custodian.
Supply only the inputs allowed for each role. No role receives final
interpretive authority.

## Required outputs

- frozen candidate and equivalence records;
- packet and context-honesty records;
- exact decoder outputs and metadata;
- canary commitments and scan booleans;
- validity records frozen before scoring;
- categorical primary scores and independent audits;
- anonymous generation-selection record;
- exposure-stratified adaptive report; and
- separately sealed validation record when later authorized.

## Stop rules

Stop on checkpoint mismatch, dirty pre-existing state, protected-identity
failure, private-source requirement, truth/scoring leakage to a decoder,
canary hit, uncontrolled tool or context exposure, response mutation,
construction or equivalence failure, parse ambiguity, invalidity,
contamination, Level 0 or Level 2 regression, score-audit failure, mapping
leak, favorable selection, attempted aggregate, third adaptive generation,
missing later authority, destructive Git requirement, or Founding Charter
conflict.

Preserve every failure. Never rerun to obtain a cleaner or more favorable
record.

## Final checks

- confirm exact authorized files and counts;
- confirm context statement and ledger fields;
- confirm all ten role boundaries;
- confirm validity-before-scoring chronology;
- confirm 12 initial, at most 9 confirmation, and 21 maximum runs;
- confirm the two-generation cap;
- confirm no aggregate, majority, winner, or favorable selection;
- confirm adaptive and sealed lanes remain separate;
- confirm real counts remain zero unless separately authorized;
- confirm protected evidence, decisions, index, and worktree scope; and
- confirm no staging, commit, push, purchase, upgrade, or paid service.
