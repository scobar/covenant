# SDA-001 — Staged Method and Blinding Protocol

> **SDA-001 v0.1 — PREPARED PRIVATE SCORING-DISAGREEMENT DIAGNOSTIC — NOT EXECUTED — NO RESCORING, COMPARISON, MAPPING REVEAL, OR REAL BBIL RUN**

This public record freezes a secrecy-safe method. Preparation was content-blind:
private scoring evidence was not semantically opened, decoded, rendered,
searched, previewed, summarized, or rescored. No diagnostic record or result
exists.

## Information-flow principles

- Two fresh cryptographically random neutral lane aliases are bound privately.
- The aliases convey no A/B label, order, mapping, outcome, exposure
  chronology, or score-freeze hint.
- Original source labels, private identities, workspace identities, execution
  order, and baseline/treatment mapping remain outside substantive role
  packets.
- Observation precedes scoring truth.
- Truth-relative diagnosis remains lane-isolated.
- Synthesis receives frozen diagnostic records, not unbounded raw evidence.
- Audit scratch freezes before the synthesis is disclosed.

## Stage 0 — Custody and lane isolation

A coordinator mechanically verifies exact identities and builds two separate
cryptographically named temporary workspaces, one per neutral lane. The
coordinator performs no substantive diagnosis and has no authority to compare
conditions.

## Stage 1 — Divergence observation before truth

Two separate fresh no-history observers run concurrently. Each receives one
lane's frozen primary record, frozen auditor scratch result, frozen audit
record, score-freeze record if present, generic lane alias, taxonomy names,
and blank observation template.

Observers receive no raw output, solution key, scorecard, other lane, mapping,
execution order, expected mechanism, desired conclusion, prior DA-001 vector,
BBIL result, or old D-035 transcript. They freeze agreement and disagreement
fields, divergence classes, provenance completeness, mechanical anomalies,
uncertainty, and alternatives without deciding who was correct or creating a
replacement vector.

## Stage 2 — Lane-isolated truth-relative diagnosis

Two different fresh no-history diagnosticians run concurrently after Stage 1
freezes. Each receives only its lane's frozen observation and authorized truth
sources. It classifies all sixteen mechanism families using only `Supported`,
`Weakly supported`, `Contradicted`, or `Unresolved`, with supporting and
weakening evidence, confounds, alternatives, and a nonrescoring boundary.

Each context receives no other lane, mapping, execution order, target result,
expected winner, public conclusion as a hint, or old transcript. It may not
assign a replacement Level 0–4 vector, choose or average judgments, amend a
frozen record, compare lanes, infer condition identity, or infer a winner.

## Stage 3 — Frozen-record cross-lane synthesis

A new fresh no-history context receives only the two frozen observations, two
frozen truth-relative mechanism records, taxonomy, aggregate D-035 public
counts, and blank synthesis template. It receives no unbounded raw evidence,
solution, scorecard, mapping, execution order, or old transcript.

The synthesis may distinguish shared, lane-specific, scorecard-wide,
record-specific, and provenance-completeness mechanisms; identify synthetic-
validation priorities; and preserve unresolved alternatives. It produces no
replacement score, condition comparison, or public lane association.

## Stage 4 — Independent scratch-before-synthesis audit

A new fresh no-history auditor receives the four frozen Stage-1/2 records and
taxonomy. Before reading the synthesis, it independently freezes its complete
sixteen-family vector, mechanism register, synthetic-validation priorities,
and unresolved alternatives.

Only then may it read the synthesis. Allowed outcomes are exactly:

```text
PASS — exact agreement
PASS WITH PRESERVED DISSENT
FAIL — diagnostic result must not freeze
```

A FAIL stops result freeze and authorizes no replacement auditor.

## Stage 5 — Private result freeze

A non-FAIL audit may permit a private result freeze binding every source and
diagnostic record. The result changes no PTR-001 score, audit, validity,
exposure, mapping, comparison, or closure record.

## Stage 6 — Separate later synthetic validation

Diagnostic execution does not itself modify an instrument or run fixtures. A
later separate decision must freeze a revised draft scoring/provenance
instrument and twelve wholly synthetic fixtures across six modules:

1. claim-inventory completeness;
2. criterion-scope interpretation;
3. threshold and categorical-boundary application;
4. query and prediction accounting;
5. evidence-to-criterion attribution; and
6. provenance-field completeness.

Each module later receives one clear control with one frozen expected
adjudication and one ambiguity control whose expected behavior is to preserve
ambiguity. Each fixture requires two fresh no-history primary adjudicators and
one different scratch-before-comparison auditor. No real Beacon output,
PTR-001 source, mapping, aggregate numeric score, or favorable selection is
allowed.

Allowed package outcomes are `READY`, `NOT READY`, and `INCONCLUSIVE` under
the frozen rules. Preparation creates no fixture and implies no real BBIL
readiness. A real BBIL candidate remains prohibited without a still-later
accepted decision after a `READY` result.

## Nonrescoring and causal boundary

Both original judgments remain equally preserved. The diagnostic cannot
select a correct score, add an adjudicator, arbitrate, average, compare,
unblind, revise the scorecard during analysis, copy output vocabulary into a
future Beacon, select a mechanism by reviewer count, normalize a missing
provenance field, or use PTR-001 to optimize a real candidate. Same-tooling
agreement is not external validation, and every future instrument change is a
hypothesis until synthetic validation passes.

No diagnostic execution, synthetic fixture, synthetic run, score change,
comparison, mapping reveal, real BBIL work, SIG-002, Levels 5–9 analysis,
higher layer, payload, carrier, distribution, or transmission follows from
this method record.
