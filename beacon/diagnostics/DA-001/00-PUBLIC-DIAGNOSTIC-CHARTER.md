# DA-001 — Public Diagnostic Charter

**PREPARED PRIVATE POST-HOC DIAGNOSTIC ANALYSIS DA-001 v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NOT EXECUTED**

## Purpose

DA-001 prepares one private, preregistered, post-hoc diagnostic analysis of
the frozen evidence from `SIG-001-HO-001-TR-001` and
`SIG-001-HO-002-TR-002`. Its purpose is to develop bounded hypotheses about
where decoding succeeded, weakened, or stopped before any successor signal is
designed.

Preparation is not analysis or evidence. DA-001 has not inspected raw evidence
under this protocol, created a diagnostic conclusion, changed a score, or
authorized a model run.

## Related Trials and Frozen Public Result

- [TR-001](../../trials/SIG-001-HO-001-TR-001/00-PUBLIC-TRIAL-CHARTER.md) remains
  closed as technical invalidity; its Fable trace remains non-scorable
  auxiliary evidence.
- [TR-002](../../trials/SIG-001-HO-002-TR-002/00-PUBLIC-TRIAL-CHARTER.md) remains
  closed with its valid-but-prior-exposed classification, five-level score,
  and dissent unchanged.
- The frozen public result remains: artificiality recovered, numeracy
  partially recovered, and framing, relations, and grammar not recovered.

DA-001 neither reopens a trial nor reinterprets that public result as a causal
finding.

## Analysis Questions

DA-001 may later ask:

1. Which observable decoding behaviors recur or diverge across the three
   frozen source records?
2. Which of sixteen preregistered diagnostic families are supported,
   weakly supported, contradicted, or unresolved relative to frozen truth?
3. Which causal hypotheses remain plausible only as future controlled
   experiment classes?
4. Which apparent explanations fail because of validity, exposure,
   interface, provider, or post-hoc confounds?

## Preregistration and Blinding

The evidence inventory, access matrix, sixteen-family hypothesis taxonomy,
templates, stage order, isolation rules, and anti-overfitting controls froze
before substantive raw-evidence inspection under DA-001. The taxonomy is
result-informed but raw-evidence-content-blind. No diagnostic family has a
current status.

## Staged Architecture

Execution, if separately begun after public commit, private backup, and custody
verification, will proceed in fixed order:

1. three source-isolated evidence-only observations;
2. three run-isolated truth-relative diagnostics after observation freeze;
3. cross-run synthesis from frozen records;
4. independent scratch-before-comparison audit; and
5. final private freeze.

Observation, truth-relative diagnosis, causal hypothesis, and design
implication remain distinct.

## Private Evidence Boundary

Raw traces, screenshots, videos, decoder responses, scoring truth, mappings,
queries, answers, reasoning, and private dissent remain private. Public records
contain method and commitment only. Provider-visible reasoning will not be
publicly transcribed.

## Immutability and Limitations

- Fable remains non-scorable.
- Sonnet RUN-001 remains technically invalid and non-scorable.
- Sonnet RUN-002 validity, score, and dissent remain immutable.
- DA-001 is post-hoc and uses the same tooling/provider family; its findings
  cannot establish an independent causal result.
- Reviewer agreement cannot select a causal story.

## Explicit Non-Authorization

DA-001 does not authorize SIG-002, signal redesign, another model run,
rescoring, a validity change, a new holdout or trial, Levels 5–9 analysis, a
higher layer, response syntax, carrier selection, distribution, transmission,
candidate preference, acceptance, canonicality, supersession, or
stabilization.
