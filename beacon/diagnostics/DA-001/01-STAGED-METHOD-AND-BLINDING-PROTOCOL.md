## Fixed Chronology and Isolation

### Stage 1 — Observation Before Truth

Three mutually isolated, fresh contexts each receive one authorized frozen
source set and a blank observation template. They receive no scoring truth,
target conclusion, other run's evidence, or other observation. Each observation
freezes before truth access.

### Stage 2 — Run-Isolated Truth-Relative Diagnosis

Three new mutually isolated contexts each receive one frozen observation, the
corresponding frozen truth sources, the preregistered taxonomy, and a blank
truth-relative template. They receive no other run's observation, evidence, or
diagnosis. Each record freezes before synthesis.

### Stage 3 — Cross-Run Synthesis

A new context receives only the three frozen observation records, three frozen
truth-relative records, the public result vector, taxonomy, and blank synthesis
template. Raw evidence is not an unbounded synthesis input.

### Stage 4 — Independent Audit

A fresh audit context independently reconstructs classifications on scratch
materials before comparing them with the frozen synthesis. Disagreements are
preserved rather than averaged away.

### Stage 5 — Freeze

The private record set freezes without changing any source evidence, score,
validity classification, or dissent.

## Interpretive Controls

- Separate observation, inference, causal hypothesis, and design implication.
- Treat correlations as hypothesis generators, not causal conclusions.
- Treat framing as one family among sixteen, not a preselected answer.
- Treat provider safeguards and interface interruption as confounds, not
  semantic results.
- Preserve same-provider, prior-exposure, invalid-run, and post-hoc limits.
- Do not select a causal story by reviewer count or copy model vocabulary into
  a future signal merely because it appeared.

## Frozen Non-Effects

This protocol produces no public diagnostic result and makes no score or
validity change. It contains no SIG-002 design, authorizes no model run, and
advances no higher layer, carrier, distribution, or transmission work.
