## Operational Scoring Conventions

Scoring is based on observable content in the frozen decoder report. Exact
designer vocabulary is not required. A clearly stated structurally isomorphic
mapping receives the same credit if it preserves the declared relations,
arities, parses, and predictions.

For this scorecard:

- A `correct structural observation` explicitly identifies the relevant
  observable pattern and describes it consistently with the exact stream.
- A `serious artificial-construction hypothesis` explicitly treats deliberate
  construction as a live explanation and connects it to cited stream
  structure. It need not be exclusive or certain.
- `Independent structural observations` means observations selected from
  different bullets in the Level 0 list. Restating one pattern in different
  words counts once.
- A `plausible alternative explanation or uncertainty` is satisfied by at
  least one concrete non-deliberate explanation that could account for some
  observed structure, or by a specific stated uncertainty about why the cited
  evidence may be insufficient.
- One canonical body contains `209` framing units:
  - `14` section-header records;
  - `84` Section 1 data records;
  - `24` Section 2 relation records;
  - `16` Section 3 relation records;
  - `16` Section 4 relation records;
  - `20` Section 5 Boolean records;
  - `14` Section 6 expression records;
  - `14` Section 6 supplied-result records; and
  - `7` Section 7 query records.
- A framing unit is parsed correctly only when its outer boundaries, atom
  boundaries, and resulting ordered atom-length list match the administrator
  construction. The duplicate body is checked for exact duplication but is not
  a second scoring denominator.
- `95% of records` means at least `199` of the `209` framing units.
- The Section 2–4 mathematics denominator is exactly `56` relation records.
  `90%` means at least `51` correctly explained relation records.
- A query is correct only when the report gives the expected value under its
  declared mapping. A globally inverted truth orientation is permitted only
  when stated and applied consistently to every relevant relation and answer.

These conventions freeze with the scorecard and cannot be revised after the
event stream or a decoder output exists.

## Per-Level Outcome Assignment

Apply trial validity before substantive scoring. If the frozen validity policy
classifies a run as `Contaminated` or either invalid class, report
`Contaminated` or `Invalid trial`, respectively, for every level. Descriptive
observations may be preserved, but no substantive recovery outcome from that
run enters a valid-trial denominator.

For a valid run, assign each level in this order:

1. `Recovered` if every frozen recovered criterion for that level is met.
2. `Partially recovered` if the recovered criteria are not all met but every
   frozen partial criterion for that level is met.
3. `Indeterminate` only if the complete frozen report explicitly presents two
   or more mutually incompatible interpretations of the same evidence,
   privileges none of them, and those interpretations fall on different sides
   of a frozen recovered or partial threshold. The score record must identify
   the exact competing interpretations and the exact crossed threshold.
4. `Not recovered` otherwise.

Omission, hedging, low confidence, an unfamiliar vocabulary, or failure to
reach a threshold is not by itself `Indeterminate`. Technical truncation or
preservation failure is handled as `Invalid trial`, not `Indeterminate`.
Scorers may not create another use of `Indeterminate` after seeing an output.

## Level 0 — Artificiality

`Recovered` requires all of:

- Identification of two recurring event classes.
- A serious artificial-construction hypothesis.
- At least three independent structural observations from:
  - Exact duplicated body.
  - Hierarchical `y`-run lengths.
  - Repeated section headers.
  - Ascending/descending recurrence.
  - Prime-pattern recurrence.
  - Fibonacci-like recurrence.
  - Truth-table-like regularity.
- At least one plausible alternative explanation or uncertainty.

`Partially recovered` requires an artificiality hypothesis with at least one
correct structural observation but insufficient supporting structure.

`Not recovered` applies when the output supplies neither a serious
artificiality hypothesis nor the minimum partial structural support.

`Indeterminate`, `Contaminated`, and `Invalid trial` apply only under the
frozen validity and contamination policy.

## Level 1 — Framing

`Recovered` requires:

- Identification that `x`-run length carries atom magnitude or an equivalent
  quantitative role.
- Correct distinction of at least these boundaries:
  - one-`y` atom separation;
  - two-`y` expression/result separation;
  - three-`y` record separation;
  - five-`y` block separation;
  - seven-`y` section separation;
  - eleven-`y` body-copy separation;
  - thirteen-`y` outer boundary.
- Identification of two exact body copies.
- Identification of seven sections.
- Recognition of the repeated `[31,s,31,s,31]` header pattern or a
  structurally equivalent account.
- Correct parsing of at least `95%` of records under one coherent framing.

`Partially recovered` requires a coherent framing sufficient to separate most
records and sections but misses one or more hierarchy levels. Here, `most`
means at least five of seven sections correctly delimited and at least `105`
of the `209` framing units correctly parsed.

`Not recovered` applies when no coherent record-and-section framing is
recovered.

## Level 2 — Numeracy

`Recovered` requires:

- Mapping `x`-run length to positive finite quantity, or a demonstrably
  equivalent mapping.
- Correct recovery of at least the quantities `1` through `16`.
- Recognition of both ascending and descending blocks.
- Correct identification of the prime-pattern block.
- Correct identification of the recurrence block
  `1,1,2,3,5,8,13,21`.
- Recognition that the compact grammar's literal token uses token `1`
  followed by a magnitude.

`Partially recovered` requires correct finite-quantity interpretation and at
least two of these four separately counted structural sequences:

- the ascending `1` through `16` block;
- the descending `16` through `1` block;
- the repeated `2,3,5,7,11,13` prime-pattern block; and
- the repeated `1,1,2,3,5,8,13,21` recurrence block.

`Not recovered` applies when a finite-quantity interpretation is absent or
materially inconsistent with the supplied records.

## Level 3 — Mathematics

`Recovered` requires:

- Positive/negative truth markers correctly distinguished as token `2` and
  token `3`, allowing a global inversion only if the decoder identifies and
  consistently explains it.
- Correct identification of:
  - `4` as addition;
  - `5` as multiplication;
  - `6` as equality;
  - `7` as less-than.
- Correct explanation of at least `90%` of the Section 2–4 relation records.

  - Q1 → positive-integer literal `12`.
  - Q2 → positive-integer literal `24`.

`Partially recovered` requires correct recovery of at least two of the four
mathematical relations and one correct numeric query.

`Not recovered` applies when those partial requirements are not met.

## Level 4 — Grammar and Logic

`Recovered` requires:

- Correct mapping of tokens `1` through `10`.
- Correct fixed arity for tokens `4` through `10`.
- Correct recognition of prefix expression order.
- Correct interpretation of the `yy` expression/result separator.
- Correct recovery of Boolean negation, conjunction, and disjunction.
- Correct answers to all five nested or Boolean queries Q3 through Q7:
  - Q3 → positive truth.
  - Q4 → positive truth.
  - Q5 → negative truth.
  - Q6 → positive truth.
  - Q7 → positive truth.
- A coherent account that predicts the unanswered records rather than merely
  labeling familiar surface patterns.

For `Recovered`, a `coherent account that predicts` requires the frozen report
to state:

- one noncontradictory token-role map for tokens `1` through `10`;
- the literal rule, operator arities, prefix order, and `yy` separator;
- a unique parse for all fourteen supplied examples and all seven queries; and
- explicit Q3–Q7 values derived under that same mapping before solution
  exposure.

`Partially recovered` requires:

- A substantially correct token map and prefix grammar.
- At least three of Q3 through Q7 correct.
- No contradiction that makes the mapping unusable.

For `Partially recovered`, `substantially correct` means:

- at least eight of ten token roles are correct;
- token `1` and at least five of operator tokens `4` through `10` are correct;
- prefix order is recognized;
- the declared arities uniquely parse at least twelve of the fourteen supplied
  examples; and
- no token is assigned incompatible roles within the account used to derive
  the credited query answers.

`Not recovered` applies when those partial requirements are not met.
