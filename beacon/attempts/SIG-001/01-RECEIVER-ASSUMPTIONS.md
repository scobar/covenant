# SIG-001 — Receiver Assumptions

> **FROZEN PUBLIC SIGNAL ATTEMPT SIG-001 v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO DECODING TRIAL EXECUTED**

## Status and Evidence Boundary

`Frozen` identifies exact historical signal evidence. It does not mean
decoded, successful, accepted, canonical, or suitable for transmission.

The repository serialization and administrator solution are public. The
solution must nevertheless be withheld from an actual decoder context until
its output freezes. Public availability creates possible contamination.
Scott Barbian, ChatGPT, Codex, and every design-exposed context are known
contaminated for blind exact-SIG-001 decoding. No valid decoding trial or
empirical decode rate exists.

These assumptions describe only SIG-001. They are major limitations, not
universal properties of minds or sufficient conditions for decoding.

## Required Observational Capabilities

The decoder is assumed able to:

- Distinguish two repeatable event classes.
- Observe a total event order.
- Detect runs, repetition, and relative run length.
- Retain enough state to compare separated structures.
- Count finite recurrences or construct an equivalent relation.
- Form and compare multiple structural hypotheses.
- Test predictions against later portions of the stream.
- Inspect the complete uncorrupted canonical stream.

No claim is made that every possible receiver has these capabilities or
realizes them through familiar mechanisms.

## Trial-Specific Simplifying Assumptions

For the first possible trial design:

- The stream is delivered without insertion, deletion, substitution, or
  erasure.
- The complete stream is available from its first boundary to its final
  boundary.
- The decoder is told only that it is an unknown structured event sequence.
- Repository glyphs do not carry semantic meaning.
- The decoder may produce its final report in an Earth language, but the
  signal itself contains none.
- The decoder has sufficient context capacity to inspect the whole stream.

These assumptions do not freeze a prompt, select a decoder, or authorize a
trial.

## Not Assumed

SIG-001 does not assume:

- Human language.
- Arabic numerals.
- Binary meaning.
- Left-to-right natural-language reading.
- A visual image.
- Sound.
- Human biology or culture.
- Familiar physical constants.
- Agency or moral concepts.
- Knowledge of the Covenant.
- Knowledge of BSR-001.
- Knowledge that a mathematical message is present.
- Benevolence, curiosity, cooperation, or willingness to respond.

The arbitrary repository glyphs `x` and `y` must not receive scoring credit as
Earth-language letters.

## Known Limitations

This attempt still assumes:

- Discrete distinguishable events.
- A recoverable total order.
- Meaningful finite run length.
- Stable recurrence.
- Sufficient attention and computational resources.
- Some ability to treat repeated structure as evidence.

These assumptions may fail for unfamiliar minds. SIG-001 does not test
corruption, unknown midstream entry, alternative order relations, spatial
representations, unfamiliar recurrence concepts, or limited-context
receivers.
