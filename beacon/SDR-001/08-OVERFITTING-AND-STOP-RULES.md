# SDR-001 — Overfitting and Stop Rules

> **SDR-001 v0.1 — NONCANONICAL SUCCESSOR-DESIGN RESEARCH — NO SIG-002 ATTEMPT, STREAM, HOLDOUT, OR MODEL TRIAL**

## Overfitting prohibitions

- Do not copy Fable or Sonnet wording, vocabulary, parsing labels, or response
  strategy into a successor design.
- Do not tune to an individual query mistake or private decoder trace.
- Do not change arithmetic teaching, grammar, or query marking together with
  framing locality.
- Do not increase the framing-cue budget and describe the increase as a
  locality effect.
- Do not remove or change duplicate-body framing in this experiment.
- Do not change provider or model configuration between paired conditions.
- Do not reuse an exposed holdout as clean evidence.
- Do not change the scorecard after stream freeze.
- Do not retry after a substantive result or because a result is unfavorable.
- Do not create an aggregate interpretation or candidate-wide winner.
- Do not claim universal decodability or undecodability.
- Do not infer a physical, agency, moral, Covenant, CSR, shared-model,
  response, carrier, distribution, or transmission result.
- Do not select among multiple exact designs after observing comparative
  results.

## Mandatory stop conditions

Stop and return to design research if:

- semantic equivalence cannot be proven;
- any of the eighteen invariants fails;
- framing-cue multiset equality cannot be proven;
- total framing-event budgets differ;
- nonframing bytes differ;
- cue strength changes with cue placement;
- a unique intended parse cannot be preserved;
- stream length or parse complexity creates an undisclosed material mismatch;
- more than one primary manipulated variable is required;
- a scorecard or denominator change would depend on a result;
- surface novelty or condition isolation cannot be established;
- a private value or raw evidence would need to be published;
- an exact signal value would need to be selected inside SDR-001; or
- completion would require a signal attempt, holdout, provider selection,
  trial, model run, higher layer, carrier, distribution, or transmission.

A pair that violates these rules must not be called a locality-only
experiment. It may be retained as a rejected or unresolved design record only
under later authorization, with the confound disclosed.

## No authority consequence

No design author, auditor, provider, model, decoder, scorer, founder, or
repository owner gains final interpretive authority from defining or applying
these rules.
