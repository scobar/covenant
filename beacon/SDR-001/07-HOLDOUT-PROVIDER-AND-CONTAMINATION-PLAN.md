# SDR-001 — Holdout, Provider, and Contamination Plan

> **SDR-001 v0.1 — NONCANONICAL SUCCESSOR-DESIGN RESEARCH — NO SIG-002 ATTEMPT, STREAM, HOLDOUT, OR MODEL TRIAL**

## Orthogonal control strategy

Framing locality is the primary manipulated variable. Provider-independent
replication is a separate replication requirement, and clean no-known-exposure
holdouts are a separate contamination requirement. Neither may be silently
changed between paired conditions or represented as the locality treatment.

## Future paired holdout requirements

- Generate a surface-novel baseline holdout and a surface-novel treatment
  holdout from the same frozen abstract semantic source.
- Preserve identical nonframing bytes.
- Preserve the same framing-cue multiset and total framing-event count.
- Blind identities to opaque condition labels.
- Keep the filled condition mapping off-repository until applicable outputs
  and complete scores freeze.
- Create an authorized public commitment before trial only under a later
  decision and without publishing sealed values.
- Where feasible, generate and commit the paired holdouts before selecting the
  decoder or provider.
- Use one fresh, isolated context per condition.

No private package, nonce, preimage, commitment, stream, mapping, or holdout is
created now.

## Provider and model controls

- Use the same provider, displayed model label, effort or configuration,
  interface, prompt, settings, and tools for both paired conditions.
- A provider or model change inside a pair requires the comparison to be
  classified as not comparable.
- Preserve exact visible UI labels while stating that hidden backend identity
  and routing may be unavailable.
- Treat Incognito as a limited interface control, not proof of provider
  isolation or absence of hidden context.
- Seek provider-independent replication after the paired primary comparison
  where feasible.
- Select no provider or model in D-029 or SDR-001.

## Exposure and denominator rules

- Prefer clean no-known-exposure evidence where feasible, while recognizing
  that `No known exposure` is not proof of no exposure.
- Do not reuse an exposed holdout as clean evidence.
- Do not count technically invalid or protocol-invalid runs in valid
  denominators.
- Preserve invalid runs and disclose their provider exposure.
- Report prior exposure, repository exposure, solution exposure, and
  same-provider dependence explicitly.
- Keep denominators exposure-stratified.
- Do not pool provider-independent replication with the primary pair without
  a later explicit analysis.

## Cost and dormancy

Authorized incremental spend is USD `0`. Any future spending requires a
separate accepted decision. If a zero-cost provider path or adequately
isolated execution is unavailable, the research may remain dormant
indefinitely without creating a substitute provider, holdout, or trial.
