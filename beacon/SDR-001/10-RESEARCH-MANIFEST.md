## Selected target and exact research question

- Selected research target: framing-cue locality.
- Lifecycle: `Research target selected — exact design not begun`.

> **Under controlled semantic equivalence and a matched framing-cue multiset, does shifting framing information from globally concentrated cues to locally repeated boundary-role cues improve recovery of the framing hierarchy without degrading artificiality detection or structured numeracy?**

This question is noncausal. Selection does not establish that framing caused
the frozen result or that a locality treatment would improve recovery.
