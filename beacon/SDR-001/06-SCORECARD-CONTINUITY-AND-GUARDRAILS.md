# SDR-001 — Scorecard Continuity and Guardrails

> **SDR-001 v0.1 — NONCANONICAL SUCCESSOR-DESIGN RESEARCH — NO SIG-002 ATTEMPT, STREAM, HOLDOUT, OR MODEL TRIAL**

## Frozen scoring philosophy

- Preserve categorical, per-level, nonaggregate scoring for Decoder Levels
  0–4.
- Treat Level 1 framing/hierarchy recovery as the primary endpoint.
- Treat Level 0 artificiality as a nonregression guardrail.
- Treat Level 2 structured numeracy as a nonregression guardrail.
- Treat Levels 3 and 4 as exploratory only.
- Keep Levels 5–9 out of scope and unscored.
- Preserve technical-validity and contamination classification as additional
  guardrails.

No result is created by this record.

## Abstract continuity of Level 1 functions

A later paired scorecard must preserve the abstract SIG-001 Level 1 functions:

1. Outer framing.
2. Duplicate bodies.
3. Section hierarchy.
4. Section ordering.
5. Header or sentinel role.
6. Delimiter-role hierarchy.
7. Record, block, and section parsing.
8. Delimiter-versus-data distinction.

The future exact design may use surface-novel realizations, but it must not
change what these abstract functions mean merely to favor one condition.

## Denominator and threshold continuity

Preserve the existing abstract framing-unit denominator and Level 1 threshold
where practical. The duplicate body remains checked under the same abstract
policy rather than silently becoming a second scoring denominator. If an
exact future design must change the denominator or threshold, a later accepted
decision must justify and freeze that change before any stream is created.
No result-dependent denominator or criterion change is permitted.

## Primary endpoint and guardrail interpretation

- Primary question: whether the treatment condition shows a material Level 1
  framing/hierarchy difference under the frozen criteria.
- Level 0 must not materially regress.
- Level 2 must not materially regress.
- Technical validity and contamination classification must remain acceptable.
- Any stream-length, framing-budget, or parse-complexity mismatch must be
  disclosed and may require a not-comparable classification.
- Levels 3 and 4 may generate later hypotheses but cannot determine the
  primary comparison.

## Allowed future comparison labels

Use only:

- `Possible improvement`
- `No material comparative difference`
- `Possible regression`
- `Unresolved comparative difference`
- `Not comparable — reason required`

These labels are condition- and endpoint-specific. They do not establish an
aggregate verdict, candidate-wide winner, universal improvement, preference,
acceptance, or supersession.
