## Exact research question

> **Under controlled semantic equivalence and a matched framing-cue multiset,
> does shifting framing information from globally concentrated cues to locally
> repeated boundary-role cues improve recovery of the framing hierarchy without
> degrading artificiality detection or structured numeracy?**

## Selection and noncausal status

Framing-cue locality is selected because it is relevant to the public Level 1
nonrecovery and to several DA-001 diagnostic sites, and because it can be
expressed as one controlled placement variable. Selection makes locality a
hypothesis-bearing research target. It does not establish that framing
locality caused the frozen result, that framing was the sole cause, or that a
local treatment would improve recovery.

The baseline family concentrates framing information primarily in global
headers, outer boundaries, and hierarchical delimiter placement. The treatment
family redistributes the same framing-cue multiset so equivalent boundary-role
information recurs adjacent to the local units it frames. Only placement or
locality may change in a later paired design.

## Relationship to the eight DA-001 experiment classes

1. Boundary-redundancy strength is deferred because changing redundancy would
   change cue strength or budget.
2. Local versus global framing is selected as the primary manipulated
   variable, with a matched cue multiset and framing-event budget.
3. Explicit versus emergent section differentiation is deferred as a later
   manipulated variable.
4. Duplicate-body cue presence versus ablation is deferred and the existing
   duplicate-body policy remains invariant.
5. Fixed framing with varied arithmetic teaching is deferred; arithmetic
   teaching remains fixed here.
6. Fixed grammar with varied query marking is deferred; grammar and query
   semantics remain fixed here.
7. Provider-independent replication is retained as an orthogonal replication
   requirement, not the primary variable.
8. Clean no-known-exposure holdout testing is retained as an orthogonal
   contamination requirement, not the primary variable.

Provider diversity and clean holdouts address validity and generalization.
They must not be substituted for, or changed within, the paired locality
comparison.
