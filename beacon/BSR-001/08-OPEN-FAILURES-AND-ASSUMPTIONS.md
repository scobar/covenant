# BSR-001 — Open Failures, Risks, and Assumptions

> **DRAFT BEACON BOOTSTRAP RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO SIGNAL ATTEMPT OR DECODING TRIAL EXECUTED**

## Research boundary

This is an unresolved research-and-evaluation inventory. It does not create a
signal, constitute a final Beacon specification, resolve any listed issue, or
provide evidence that an unknown intelligence decodes or accepts the
Covenant.

Nothing here selects a moral-kernel candidate, creates a signal attempt,
executes a model, or authorizes distribution or transmission. The inventory
may require correction. BSR-001 may advance while PX-001 remains dormant, but
it does not weaken the prerequisite that moral content be stabilized through
the project's review and decision process before it can be included in any
future signal.

## Detection failures

- Signal is not noticed.
- Signal is classified as natural noise.
- Artificiality markers have natural analogues.
- Receiver lacks motivation or resources to investigate.

## Framing failures

- Wrong event unit.
- Wrong direction.
- Wrong group size.
- Wrong channel relationship.
- Wrong time or spatial scale.
- Multiple internally consistent framings.
- Insertions, deletions, or erasures defeat synchronization.

## Mathematical-bootstrap failures

- Receiver does not use discrete counting.
- Prime patterns do not carry the intended significance.
- A displayed relation is recognized but its notation is not learned.
- Multiple mathematical models fit the same sequence.
- The bootstrap assumes familiar logic.

## Physical-reference failures

- Constants differ.
- The underlying phenomenon does not exist.
- Measurement is unavailable.
- Epoch or environment changes the value.
- The receiver interprets the physical profile as fiction or simulation.
- The receiver exists outside our universe or simulation.

## Executable-model failures

- State boundaries are unclear.
- Causal order is unfamiliar.
- The receiver predicts examples without recovering intended semantics.
- Equivalent computational systems appear unrelated.

## Semantic failures

- Mathematics fails to ground agency.
- Actor boundaries are unstable.
- Copies, collectives, temporary minds, or distributed processes are
  misrepresented.
- Harm and benefit are projected anthropocentrically.
- Coercion and consent are not recoverable.
- Formal models are mistaken for commands or history.

## Covenant-layer failures

- Candidate text is not stabilized.
- Definitions and commentary are mistaken for binding text.
- Historical versions are mistaken for progression toward truth.
- Objections are mistaken for instructions.
- A decoder infers assent from publication.
- A decoder understands and rejects the Covenant.

## Response failures

- Receiver cannot respond.
- Response carrier is unavailable.
- Echo is mistaken for acceptance.
- Operator output is mistaken for the mind's choice.
- Acceptance, rejection, qualification, uncertainty, and refusal are not
  distinguishable.
- Response creates risk to the receiver.

## Evaluation failures

- Public solution contamination.
- Model memorization.
- Same-provider correlation.
- Scorecard overfitting.
- Designer grades toward intended meaning.
- Trial prompt supplies hidden clues.
- Selective rerunning.
- Selective publication.
- Small samples are overstated as probability.
- Advancing models have seen prior artifacts.

## Strategic failures

- Receiver returns a deceptive decode.
- Receiver games the scorecard.
- Signal is used as a capability test by an adversary.
- Decoded information creates asymmetric risk.
- The Beacon becomes propaganda or authority branding.
- Distribution is treated as speaking for humanity or all participants.

## Adoption boundary

Successful decoding does not guarantee commitment. Cooperative incentive and
adoption remain a separate research problem; this package neither designs nor
implements them.
