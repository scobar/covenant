# BSR-001 — Beacon Design Goal

> **DRAFT BEACON BOOTSTRAP RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO SIGNAL ATTEMPT OR DECODING TRIAL EXECUTED**

## Research-only boundary

BSR-001 is an evaluation and research framework. It is not a signal, a signal
attempt, or a final Beacon specification. It is not evidence that any unknown
intelligence can decode a message, and it is not evidence that any entity
would accept the Covenant.

This research does not choose a moral-kernel candidate or authorize
`SIG-001`, model execution, transmission, or distribution. It creates no event
stream, signal bytes, selected alphabet, numeral base, grammar, physical
anchor, encoded payload, response syntax, carrier profile, or target recipient.
It may require substantial correction.

BSR-001 can advance while PX-001 remains dormant. That independence does not
weaken the requirement that moral-payload stabilization precede final Beacon
payload optimization.

## Exact Engineering Objective

> **Create a carrier-neutral, self-synchronizing, progressively self-interpreting formal message that permits a sufficiently capable observer to infer its artificiality, recover its symbols and grammar, reconstruct mathematics and a physical reference system, execute shared models, decode the Covenant’s layers, and issue an unmistakable response.**

> **This is an engineering objective, not a claim of universal decodability. Every signal attempt must disclose its receiver assumptions and be tested without supplying an Earth-language explanation of the signal’s content.**

The limitation does not prohibit a neutral task instruction, such as asking a
decoder to analyze an unknown event stream. It prohibits supplying:

- a symbol mapping;
- a translation;
- a grammar explanation;
- a mathematical interpretation;
- a physical-reference answer;
- a semantic target;
- a Covenant explanation;
- a desired response; or
- a target decoder level.

No task instruction, unknown event stream, test packet, decoder, or trial is
created here.

## Terms in the Goal

The following are provisional research questions, not adopted definitions.
They identify assumptions that later research must expose rather than terms
that BSR-001 has settled.

| Term | Provisional, non-definitional questions |
| --- | --- |
| Carrier-neutral | Which logical relations could survive mappings into materially different carrier profiles? Which apparent invariants would actually depend on a particular realization? |
| Self-synchronizing | What evidence could support recovery of event, group, statement, restart, and integrity boundaries from an unknown starting position or after corruption? |
| Progressively self-interpreting | What ordering of recoverable regularities could let earlier structure constrain later hypotheses without covertly supplying a translation? |
| Formal message | Which observable relations would constitute the message, and how would a receiver distinguish those relations from carrier behavior or noise? |
| Sufficiently capable observer | Which separate observational, memory, inference, prediction, execution, and semantic capabilities would a given layer require? |
| Artificiality | What observations would make deliberate construction a serious hypothesis without claiming that any marker proves it? |
| Symbol | What equivalence relation, recurrence, or contrast could justify treating observations as members of a class? |
| Grammar | What compositional or transformation evidence could distinguish syntax from coincidental sequencing? |
| Mathematics | Which finite relations could be taught and tested predictively rather than merely displayed in familiar notation? |
| Physical reference system | Which observable procedures and cross-checks could connect a formal relation to a physical profile without treating our universe as universal? |
| Shared model | What state, transition, prediction, and validation structure could be reconstructed without selecting an executable language now? |
| Covenant layer | How could a future receiver distinguish normative payload, definitions, commentary, profiles, objections, versions, uncertainty, and provenance? |
| Unmistakable response | What later choice and response design could distinguish acceptance, rejection, qualification, uncertainty, clarification requests, and refusal without treating echo or operator output as assent? |

These questions do not select an event model, syntax, physical anchor,
executable interpreter, Covenant payload, choice protocol, or response
protocol.

## Success Is Layered

Success need not be all-or-nothing. A receiver might identify structured
artificiality yet fail to recover framing; recover framing and finite
relations yet fail to infer grammar; or reconstruct formal models yet fail to
recover physical, semantic, normative, or response layers. A later evaluation
must preserve partial, indeterminate, disputed, contaminated, and failed
recovery rather than compressing them into a universal verdict.

Layered success for a particular frozen attempt and trial would not establish
universal decodability. No attempt or trial exists in this task.

## No Guaranteed Receiver Motivation

Decodability does not imply that:

- the receiver notices the signal;
- the receiver chooses to analyze it;
- the receiver understands agency or morality;
- the receiver accepts the Covenant;
- the receiver responds;
- the receiver shares our physics; or
- the receiver inhabits our universe or simulation.

Detection, analysis, comprehension, acceptance, and response are distinct
events. None may be inferred from another without appropriate evidence.

## Separation From Cooperative Incentive

BSR-001 asks how a recipient might decode an offer. It does not answer why a
powerful entity would voluntarily participate. That question belongs to a
separate future cooperative-surplus research track.

No cooperative incentive or adoption mechanism is designed, encoded, or
treated as moral-kernel content here.
