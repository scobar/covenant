# BSR-001 — Mathematical and Physical Anchor Research

> **DRAFT BEACON BOOTSTRAP RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO SIGNAL ATTEMPT OR DECODING TRIAL EXECUTED**

## Research boundary

This document records candidate anchor families and evaluation questions for
research and evaluation only. It does not select an anchor, record
authoritative numerical values, create a signal, constitute a final Beacon
specification, or provide evidence that an unknown intelligence decodes or
accepts the Covenant.

Nothing here selects a moral-kernel candidate, creates a signal attempt,
executes a model, or authorizes distribution or transmission. These research
questions may require correction. BSR-001 may advance while PX-001 remains
dormant, but it does not weaken the prerequisite that moral content be
stabilized through the project's review and decision process before it can be
included in any future signal.

## Formal mathematical branch

Candidate families for research are:

- Finite counting.
- Succession.
- Equality and inequality.
- Addition.
- Multiplication.
- Prime and composite structure.
- Ratios.
- Geometric invariants.
- Symmetry.
- Recursion.
- Logical validity.
- Computable state transformations.
- Predictive examples and withheld continuations.

Each family must be evaluated by asking:

- What receiver assumption does it require?
- Could a natural process produce the pattern?
- Can it teach notation rather than merely display a pattern?
- Can the decoder predict unseen examples?
- Can it bootstrap a more compact representation?
- What alternative interpretations remain?
- Does it assume discrete objects?
- Does it assume linear order?
- Does it assume finite arithmetic?

## Physical-reference branch

Candidate families for research are:

- Dimensionless physical constants.
- Particle or system mass ratios.
- Atomic or molecular transition-frequency ratios.
- Spectral-line ratios.
- Particle lifetime ratios.
- Reproducible transition periods.
- Astronomical-period ratios.
- Decay-process ratios.
- Geometry and propagation relations.

No numerical value is selected or recorded here.

Each future physical-anchor record must state:

- Formal definition.
- Measurement or reproduction procedure.
- Dimensional or dimensionless status.
- Unit convention, if any.
- Expected uncertainty.
- Redundant cross-check.
- Possible degeneracy.
- Environmental dependence.
- Epoch dependence.
- Whether it could differ elsewhere.
- Whether it could fail outside our universe or simulation.
- What a decoder could observe rather than merely be told.

## Separation rule

- The formal mathematical core must remain distinguishable from the
  our-universe physical profile.
- Failure to share our physical constants must not automatically make the
  formal branch undecodable.
- The physical profile must identify itself as a profile, not universal truth.

## Constants are not semantics

- Mathematical or physical constants may verify an interpretation.
- They do not by themselves identify symbol boundaries.
- They do not by themselves teach grammar.
- They do not by themselves define agency, harm, autonomy, cooperation, or
  moral obligation.
- Ethical-semantic grounding will require formal models and contrast cases.
