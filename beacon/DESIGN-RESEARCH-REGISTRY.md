## Nonexecution boundary

SDR-001 remains frozen historical research at the target-selection gate.
SDR-002 selects `Nested local framing signatures` through frozen feasibility
hard gates and privately freezes one exact symbolic baseline/treatment pair.
The exact maps remain private, and all serialization, implementation, and
execution counts remain zero. Provider-independent replication and clean
no-known-exposure holdouts remain future validity controls rather than the
primary manipulated variable.

A later accepted decision is required for deterministic surface serialization.
Separate later decisions are required for attempt creation, holdout creation,
trial preparation, and execution. No attempt follows automatically. No higher
layer, carrier, distribution, or transmission follows.
