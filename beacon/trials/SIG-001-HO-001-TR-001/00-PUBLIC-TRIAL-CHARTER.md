## Evidence chronology

Private custody-integrity verification is the custodian's private,
nonrevealing recomputation that sealed files, manifests, preimages, and
commitments still match. It is required during preparation and immediately
before execution. It reveals no private content publicly, supplies no mapping,
solution, scorecard, preimage, private identity, or holdout content to the
decoder or pre-output-freeze scoring context, is not reveal-stage
verification, and creates no decoding result.

Reveal-stage commitment verification occurs only after the raw decoder output
and complete score have frozen. Under later authorization, the relevant
private preimages, manifests, mapping, solution, and scorecard materials are
revealed and compared with their public commitments. Every mismatch or failed
verification is preserved. Public recording requires separate authorization,
and verification cannot retroactively change the frozen output or score.

The required chronology is:

1. Freeze private configuration and packet.
2. Perform private custody-integrity verification.
3. Publish and commit the public preparation package.
4. Reconfirm private custody-integrity verification immediately before
   execution.
5. Execute exactly one permitted decoder run.
6. Freeze the complete raw output and execution metadata.
7. Perform validity classification and scoring.
8. Freeze the complete score.
9. Reveal the authorized private preimages, manifests, mapping, solution, and
   scorecard materials.
10. Perform reveal-stage commitment verification.
11. Preserve every mismatch, deviation, failure, and alternative
    interpretation.
12. Consider publication only through a later explicit decision.

Output is not supplied to ChatGPT before score freeze. No private solution or
mapping may be supplied to Claude. No decoder conversation was opened or used
and no input was supplied during preparation.

## Limitations and nonclaims

This trial is contamination-reduced, not proven contamination-free.
Promotional credits avoid incremental project spending but do not create
independent evaluator diversity. This is one same-provider, same-interface
model datapoint. One run cannot establish a universal probability.

A successful lower-level decode would not establish Decoder Levels 5–9. A
failed decode would not establish universal undecodability. No candidate,
Covenant, CSR, physical, agency, choice, response, carrier, distribution, or
transmission layer is included or follows. No participant has final
interpretive authority.
