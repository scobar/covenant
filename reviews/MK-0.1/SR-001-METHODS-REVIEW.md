## Outcome Blindness and Scenario Selection

The AC-004–AC-006 procedure meaningfully reduces one source of bias: their
evaluators did not receive target results, and AC-006 did not receive AC-003's
result before freezing its own substantive evaluation. This supports describing
their outcomes as derived rather than prescribed.

Outcome blindness does not remove selection effects upstream:

- The same project collaboration selected the candidate, framework, scenario
  universe, scenario facts, questions, and evaluation boundaries.
- SC-004 is a safeguard-rich near-neighbor designed to test a less-coercive
  integration arrangement.
- SC-005 stipulates genuine scarcity and public challenge to test whether
  costly refusal and temporary restrictions can be distinguished from
  coercion.
- SC-006 intentionally reproduces AC-003's material scenario conditions for
  bounded reanalysis.
- The scenario designer knew the earlier hypotheses when selecting all three
  countertests, even though the evaluators did not receive target outcomes.
- Every scenario is hypothetical and embeds facts that materially structure
  the plausible interpretations. Different stipulations could change the
  result without changing the candidate.

The second batch therefore supplies outcome-blind conditional reasoning under
purpose-selected inputs. It is stronger than prescribed conclusions for
testing whether a result can be derived without a target, but weaker than
independently selected and independently evaluated corroboration.

## AC-003 and AC-006

AC-003 and AC-006 are scenario-equivalent, not two distinct samples. Their
relationship is properly recorded as `Partial same-tooling convergence`.

The substantive agreement is limited but material: both identify a hostile
formal-compliance path involving essential-resource dependency, assimilation,
and copy-based exit, and both reach the same case-specific overall outcome.
The comparison also records substantial divergence. Seven dimension outcomes
agree and eight differ; AC-006 treats several dimensions as surviving with
reservation where AC-003 records failure, differs on identity and layer
placement, and uses a different diagnostic mix.

This pattern supports the narrower claim that the core hostile-compliance
hypothesis survived an outcome-blind same-tooling reanalysis. It weakens a
broader claim that all implicated protections failed or that the first
evaluation was replicated in full. The repeated overall label is not a second
vote, and the common tooling, framework, selected scenario, and project
context prevent independent corroboration.

## Evidence Synthesis Without Aggregation

The records support several distinct observations without being converted
into a tally:

- AC-001 supplies a prescribed hypothesis that MK-0.1 can direct precaution
  toward a possible communication-limited moral patient while leaving entity
  boundaries and thresholds unresolved.
- AC-002 supplies a prescribed disputed case in which protective intervention
  and conflicted authority remain simultaneously plausible.
- AC-003 supplies a prescribed severe failure hypothesis concerning
  dependency-conditioned assimilation and nominal exit.
- AC-004 independently derives, within the same tooling family, survival with
  reservation under unusually strong stipulated safeguards.
- AC-005 independently derives, within the same tooling family, a
  hostile-compliance failure even under stipulated genuine scarcity and
  procedural safeguards, while preserving dissent about the severity and
  remedy.
- AC-006 independently derives, within the same tooling family and before
  comparison with AC-003, a narrower version of AC-003's core failure while
  preserving substantial dimension-level disagreement.

The pattern is informative because it is not uniform. The records expose
conditional strengths, disputed authority questions, and hostile
exploitability concerns. That variation is more useful for hypothesis
generation than a forced common outcome would be. It still cannot estimate
how often MK-0.1 succeeds, fails, or is disputed across an independently
defined case universe.

## Perspective Contact Is Not Meaningful Coverage

The matrix records contact with several named perspective categories and
direct contact with every clause. It also records all clause pairs as
contacted within multi-clause evaluations. These are indexing facts, not
coverage findings.

Meaningful coverage remains unestablished because:

- Nonhuman animals, extraterrestrial civilizations, and creators,
  simulators, or apparently external intelligences receive no direct contact.
- Modeling a category does not supply testimony, representation, assent, or an
  evaluator drawn from that category.
- Clause 1 has comparatively narrow direct contact.
- Pair contact usually occurs inside a larger clause system rather than in an
  isolated interaction test.
- Contact does not establish the strongest plausible hostile reading of each
  clause separately or of every materially relevant interaction.
- Deletion and compression, plain-language paraphrase, formally designated
  non-sentient powerful-optimizer, and unfamiliar-reality or
  external-simulator tests remain unused as primary test families.
- Material distinctness, case quality, interaction completeness, and
  evaluator diversity remain unresolved.

D-009's proposed quantity rule is therefore not demonstrated as complete, and
the architectural note correctly identifies why quantity alone would be
insufficient. This review neither accepts nor changes D-009.

## Stipulation and Empirical-Evidence Limits

The records reason from designed premises. Their conclusions are appropriately
conditional on stipulated alternatives, avoidability, scarcity, risk,
continuity, mortality, resource control, review mechanisms, and enforcement.
This is valid for conceptual stress testing, but the stipulations cannot serve
as evidence that the described facts hold in an application.

No supplied record contains participant evidence, behavioral observation,
implementation results, measured institutional performance, verified
continuity, calibrated risk estimates, or tests of trustee and review
independence. Consequently, the records can expose possible textual and
architectural failure modes, but cannot establish their real-world frequency,
severity, feasibility, or remedy effectiveness.

## Severe Failure and Stabilization

One severe, plausible, unresolved Protective Insufficiency or Hostile
Exploitability finding can justify withholding provisional stabilization while
the failure is investigated. This follows the framework's noncompensation
posture: strength elsewhere should not conceal a severe protective failure.

That hold is not a candidate-wide verdict. It does not establish that every
clause fails, that the candidate should be rejected, or that a particular
remedy is correct. Here, the most severe findings are conceptually material
but same-tooling, selection-dependent, and stipulation-bound. They justify a
blocker inquiry and focused attempts to reproduce or localize the problem, not
a claim of final invalidity.

D-009 remains Proposed. Its architectural note leaves blocker status,
material distinctness, case quality, clause interactions, and evaluator
diversity unresolved. The paragraph above is a provisional methodological
implication only; it does not accept, amend, or complete D-009.

## Exploratory Drafting Versus Stabilization

Exploratory drafting does not need the same threshold as provisional
stabilization. A clearly labeled successor draft can function as a hypothesis
or test instrument. Stabilization, by contrast, is an evidential and lifecycle
claim that should require materially broader, more independent, and
higher-quality support.

The supplied records identify sufficiently concrete revision hypotheses for
exploration: formal versus meaningful choice, controller self-certification,
the force of cross-clause protections, continuity and exit, and the boundary
between kernel protection and operational layers. Drafting against those
hypotheses would not establish that the draft is better, accepted, or ready
for stabilization. MK-0.1 must remain unchanged and traceable.

## What the Records Justify Next

- **Exploratory successor work:** Justified as hypothesis generation, not as
  resolution or advancement.
- **Targeted evaluation:** Justified to test Clause 1 more deeply, unused test
  families, missing perspectives, isolated interactions, and independently
  selected controls before any stabilization claim.
- **External review:** Justified to obtain evidence unavailable from
  same-tooling repetition. It is necessary before claiming independent
  corroboration, though not a prerequisite for producing an exploratory test
  artifact.
- **Non-kernel layer experiments:** Justified to test whether definitions,
  profiles, choice procedures, evidence rules, or governance can close
  identified loopholes without transferring substantive protection into
  optional or controller-selected layers.
- **Candidate-wide conclusion:** Not justified. The records do not select a
  universal survival, failure, acceptance, rejection, or stabilization result.

## Minimum Additional Evidence

Before a stabilization decision, the minimum additional evidence should
include:

1. At least one genuinely external human or model review with disclosed
   materials, evaluator identity class, and no target outcome.
2. Independently selected scenarios or a preregistered selection rule, so the
   collaboration that authored the candidate and earlier hypotheses does not
   exclusively determine the tested cases.
3. Clause-specific strongest-hostile readings for every clause and targeted
   tests of materially important clause interactions, including interactions
   currently contacted only inside larger systems.
4. Direct work on the uncontacted perspective categories and the unused
   primary test families, while preserving that modeling is not
   representation.
5. A replication of the AC-003/AC-006 core hypothesis using different
   evaluator tooling or external reviewers, with comparison only after the
   new result is frozen.
6. Layer-isolation experiments applying the same frozen scenarios to explicit
   kernel-only and auxiliary-layer conditions, with outcome criteria fixed in
   advance. This would help locate failures without assuming that every
   operational gap is a wording defect.
7. Empirical evidence before making any application claim about consent,
   continuity, scarcity, risk, institutional independence, review,
   enforcement, or real-world feasibility.

These items are not presented as a complete stabilization rule and do not
replace the unresolved decision process for D-009.

## Primary Recommendation

**Recommend exploratory successor drafting.**

The confidence level is **moderate**. The evidence is strong enough to define
testable revision hypotheses and weak enough that any draft must remain
noncanonical, outcome-uncertain, and unsuitable for stabilization without
additional evidence. The drafting exercise should be evaluated against frozen
counterexamples, new targeted cases, layer-isolation experiments, and external
review; it should not be treated as the remedy merely because it was produced.

**Preserved dissent:** A stricter methods reviewer could reasonably defer all
successor text until layer-isolation experiments or external evaluation better
locate the failure. That position is credible because the present records do
not resolve whether the central problems are kernel force, definitions,
profiles, protocols, evidence, governance, or shared evaluator priors. This
review nevertheless gives exploratory drafting priority because hypothesis
generation has a lower evidential threshold than stabilization and the
supplied batch already exposes concrete, conflicting tests for a future draft.

No successor language is drafted, paraphrased, or proposed in this review.
