> **ARCHITECT-SPECIFIED EXPLORATORY DESIGN RECORD v0.1 — NONCANONICAL**

This record documents the design hypothesis embodied in exact Candidate
MK-0.2. It does not evaluate the candidate or convert a drafting objective into
an achieved result.

  acceptance or preference.
- ChatGPT's authorship does not grant interpretive authority or make the design

validation, review, publication, and agreement remain distinct roles. No

## Architectural Resolution

- Exploratory drafting has a lower evidence threshold than preference,
  supersession, acceptance, recommendation, or stabilization.
- SR-001's primary recommendation is accepted only for hypothesis generation.
- SR-001's secondary recommendation remains a binding process constraint.
- Targeted evaluation is required before any comparative preference,
  recommendation, supersession, or stabilization consideration.
- MK-0.1 remains immutable, under adversarial review, and not superseded.
- No result transfers from MK-0.1 to MK-0.2.
- Creating MK-0.2 does not establish that an MK-0.1 weakness was correctly
  localized or that MK-0.2 improves it.

Recurrent protective signals include precaution under moral-status
uncertainty, anti-entitlement under capability asymmetry, bounded protective
intervention, protection against avoidable irreversible loss, corrigibility,
voluntary cooperation, meaningful exit, and protection independent of
acceptance. Recurrent hostile-compliance hypotheses concern self-certification,
formal rather than meaningful choice, the scope and force of Clause 5, nominal
exit, and nominal acceptance-independent protection.

The evidence basis is limited. Clause 1 has weak direct contact. AC-003 and
AC-006 use scenario-equivalent conditions and are not independent
replications. All case and SR-001 work is same-tooling, and no external
evaluator diversity or empirical validation is present.

## Design Invariants

The successor design was required to preserve visibly:

- Uncertainty is not permission for exploitation, domination, or avoidable
  destruction.
- Capability does not create greater moral worth or rightful authority.
- Greater power increases responsibility for restraint and protection.
- Meaningful agency and voluntary choice remain protected.
- Serious protective intervention remains possible under bounded conditions.
- Avoidable irreversible loss and present disposability remain constrained.
- Honesty, verification, challenge, correction, cooperation, and exit remain
  visible.
- Protection remains independent of comprehension, acceptance, and
  reciprocity.
- Refusal and rejection remain distinguishable and non-coerced.
- Auxiliary layers cannot become the sole source of these protections.

These are design invariants, not findings that the new wording preserves them
successfully in every interpretation or application.
