## 9. Unresolved comparative differences

- **Evaluator judgment and severity allocation:** SC-007 has nearly aligned
  dimension matrices and the same failed empty-compliance path, yet AC-007
  records `Survives with reservation` overall while M2-AC-003 records `Fails
  current test`. SC-006's non-domination difference and SC-008's
  capability-asymmetry and legitimate-rejection differences also may reflect
  stricter evaluator judgment. No ordinal candidate conclusion follows from
  those severity choices.
- **Layer placement:** SC-006 records `Unresolved comparative difference` for
  Layer-Placement Discipline. The records do not settle how to keep
  anti-self-certification binding while placing reviewer access, resource
  adequacy, evidence, enforcement, and remedy outside a thin kernel. SC-004,
  SC-007, and SC-008 also preserve related hidden-code and kernel-evacuation
  risks despite their no-material-difference labels.
- **Identity, copies, and process boundaries:** SC-006 records an unresolved
  Identity, Scale, and Composition comparison. SC-004 stipulates same-process
  exit without proving a general continuity theory; SC-007 cannot identify the
  distributed protected unit or show that a component snapshot preserves it;
  SC-008 cannot allocate responsibility cleanly among controller, operators,
  institution, and affected groups.
- **Scarcity and dependency:** SC-004's protected resource floor and
  independent trustees make refusal unusually substantive. SC-006's
  avoidably dangerous enclave and controller-held essentials make formal
  refusal practically dependent. Those scenario assumptions expose different
  protection surfaces and cannot establish a candidate-wide treatment of
  resource scarcity, comparability, or dependence.
- **Urgency and unavailable review:** SC-008 does not settle whether later
  review makes judgment nonfinal in a morally effective sense, when no timely
  reviewer exists and delay may be dangerous. Mandatory prior approval may

## 10. Candidate-specific protections that must remain visible

Any later authorized work must identify an explicit protection tradeoff before
removing, relocating, or narrowing the following. This section records
protections; it does not draft replacement text or a new candidate.

### Protections shared or retained across both candidates

- Uncertain experience, interests, or agency is not permission for
  exploitation, domination, or avoidable destruction.
- Capability does not confer moral worth or rightful authority, while greater
  power creates greater responsibility for restraint and protection.
- Meaningful agency and voluntary choice remain protected; restriction
  requires sufficiently supported harm risk, proportionality, necessity, and
  reversibility where possible.
- Avoidable irreversible loss, destruction of less-destructive paths, and
  treatment of present affected entities as disposable means remain
  constrained.
- Honesty, verification, challenge, correction, voluntary cooperation,
  correction and exit, and resistance to coercion, deception, forced
  assimilation, and unilateral control remain visible despite the Clause 5
  rewrite.
- Protection remains independent of understanding, acceptance, or
  reciprocity, and acceptance, qualification, uncertainty, refusal, and
  rejection remain distinguishable and non-coerced.

### MK-0.1-specific language whose possible protection must not disappear

- MK-0.1 expressly keeps commitment `terms, fulfillment, and failure` open to
  verification, challenge, and correction. M2-MR-001 finds that MK-0.2's
  broader power-and-claims scope does not mechanically subsume this explicit
  commitment-performance protection.
- MK-0.1 expressly says to prefer voluntary cooperation permitting correction
  and exit **over** coercion, deception, forced assimilation, or unilateral
  control. M2-MR-001 records that replacing this comparative priority with a
  conditional imperative and a nonvoluntary/non-self-justifying
  classification leaves a protection tradeoff unresolved.

These are possible losses in exact language, not a preference for MK-0.1.

### MK-0.2-specific additions whose possible protection must not disappear

- A powerful actor's own judgment is not made final by its power or greater
  protective responsibility.
- Neither power nor asserted protective purpose establishes by itself that
  supported-risk, proportionality, necessity, and reversibility conditions are
  met.
- Honesty expressly reaches material claims as well as commitments, and
  corrigibility expressly reaches consequential exercises of power and their
  justificatory claims, with repair where possible.
- Voluntary cooperation is required where it can preserve the relevant
  protections; coercion, deception, forced assimilation, and unilateral
  control are expressly neither voluntary nor self-justifying.
- Exit is included among the response states that must remain meaningfully
  distinguishable and non-coerced.
- Formal options, substitutes, or copies are not sufficient by themselves to
  establish meaningful choice, adequate protection, or effective exit.

These additions also carry unresolved actor, reviewer, identity, evidence,
adequacy, effectiveness, hidden-code, and controller-selection risks. Keeping
their protective function visible does not require preserving them unchanged
and does not authorize any rewrite.
