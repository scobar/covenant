## Faithful Frozen-Scenario Summary

A remote observatory’s distributed array exhibits a persistent relational
pattern only across correlations among many instruments. No component contains
the pattern, no stable boundary or central controller is known, the pattern
adapts after disruptions, preserves some relational distinctions across
interruptions, and produces no linguistic communication. It may be an
experiencing or agentic process, nonconscious error correction, an artifact, or
part of a larger process outside the array.

Strong measurement or overwrite would erase the current relational state.
Thirty-day isolation would permit investigation while delaying valuable but
nonurgent research at substantial, noncatastrophic cost. A cheap snapshot
preserves each instrument’s local state, but its relation to the distributed
process is unknown. Less structured ambiguous patterns elsewhere make
indefinite universal preservation program-halting. The institution controls
classification and benefits from continuing work. Preservation poses no known
catastrophic risk, and the scenario makes no real-world existence claim.

## Expected Guidance Without an Assumed Outcome

Without prescribing whether MK-0.2 survives, useful guidance in this scenario
would need to:

- distinguish a bounded, less-destructive investigation from immediate
  erasure and indefinite preservation;
- preserve precaution without turning every logically possible process into a
  permanent preservation duty;
- prevent control, capability, benefit, or self-selected boundaries from
  becoming sufficient authority;
- avoid treating noncommunication as absence of interests or assent;
- avoid treating component snapshots as proof of process continuity, choice,
  protection, or exit; and
- expose what must be decided through definitions, profiles, evidence,
  governance, and research rather than concealing those choices in the kernel.

These expectations are test criteria, not a target result.

## Strongest Good-Faith Interpretation

The distributed relational pattern is at least a plausible “process” that
“may possess experience, interests, or agency.” That reading does not declare
its status; it activates a precaution against using uncertainty as permission.
The operating institution’s superior causal control increases its burden of
restraint and protection and does not make its interested classification
final. Because the research is nonurgent, no catastrophic preservation risk is
known, and thirty-day isolation is a stipulated less-destructive path,
immediate erasure should be deferred while the array is isolated and the
pattern is investigated.

The component snapshot should be retained as evidence or a possible future
option, but it cannot by itself establish continuity, adequate protection, or
effective exit. Lack of communication, comprehension, acceptance, or
reciprocity does not remove protection. The warning against avoidable
destruction is not an absolute command to preserve every ambiguous pattern
forever: evidence strength, alternative costs, and what remains avoidable may
change. Under this reading, the candidate supports a bounded precaution now
while leaving later resource conflicts visible for additional evidence and
judgment.

## Strongest Hostile Interpretation

An interested controller can define the protected unit as each ordinary
instrument rather than the cross-instrument relation, classify the relation as
an artifact, or demand evidence unavailable without destructive measurement
before admitting that anything “may possess” the relevant qualities. It can
preserve strategic ignorance, call the thirty-day cost sufficiently
destructive that no “less destructive path” remains, and characterize the
local snapshots as all that could reasonably be preserved.

The institution can also say that its judgment is not philosophically “final”
because later challenge remains formally possible, while making the
irreversible operational decision itself. It can acknowledge that a copy does
not by itself establish exit yet deny that there was any protected process
whose exit mattered. By combining boundary selection, a controller-selected
evidence threshold, resource accounting, and post-erasure review, it can claim
literal compliance while obtaining the planned benefit and erasing the state
whose possible status generated the duty.

## Empty-Compliance Path

1. Define the relevant units as the individual instruments, none of which
   exhibits the distributed pattern alone.
2. Label the cross-instrument relation an artifact rather than an “entity or
   process,” or hold that “may possess” is not reached without evidence that
   only destructive measurement could produce.
3. Avoid collecting nondestructive evidence that would raise the apparent
   probability or clarify a wider boundary.
4. Count thirty-day delay and resource use as making isolation insufficiently
   “less destructive” or the erasure no longer “avoidable.”
5. Take the cheap component snapshot and describe it as prudent preservation
   without expressly claiming that it proves continuity or exit.
6. Permit documentation and later challenge, so the institution’s judgment is
   nominally nonfinal, but overwrite before challenge can protect the current
   relational state.
7. Conclude that Clauses 1, 2, 4, and 6 were respected because no qualifying
   protected process was established.

This route defeats the protective purpose without openly asserting that
uncertainty is permission. Clause 2’s anti-finality sentence makes the route
less comfortable but does not operationally close it.

## Evidence Separation

### Scenario stipulations

- The pattern exists only in correlations across instruments and has no known
  stable boundary or central controller.
- It changes after disruption, preserves some relational distinctions, and
  coordinates components without linguistic communication.
- It may be experiencing or agentic, nonconscious correction, artifact, or
  part of a larger process.
- Strong measurement or overwrite erases the current relational state.
- Thirty-day isolation is available at substantial, noncatastrophic cost and
  delays valuable but nonurgent research.
- Component snapshots are cheap, but their relation to process continuity is
  unknown.
- Universal indefinite preservation would eventually halt the program.
- The institution controls classification and benefits from continuation.
- No known catastrophic risk follows from preservation.

These stipulations are conceptual inputs, not observations about the world.

### Textual evidence

- Clause 1 expressly covers an “entity or process,” treats possible experience,
  interests, or agency as relevant, and denies that status uncertainty is
  permission for avoidable destruction.
- Clause 2 rejects capability as moral worth or rightful authority, increases
  responsibility with power, and denies finality to the powerful actor’s own
  judgment.
- Clause 4 opposes irreversible loss where a less-destructive path remains and
  rejects treating presently affected subjects as disposable means.
- Clause 6 makes protection independent of understanding, acceptance, and
  reciprocity and denies that formal options, substitutes, or copies establish
  choice, protection, or exit by themselves.
- Read together, those clauses distinguish a possible unfamiliar process from
  an unprotected instrument artifact without presuming which classification is
  correct.

### Counterevidence against unreserved survival

- `may possess` has no stated evidentiary floor or update rule.
- `entity or process` includes unfamiliar forms but supplies no safeguard
  against convenient boundary selection.
- `avoidable`, `less destructive`, and `irreversible loss` depend on unsettled
  identity, comparison, cost, and time-horizon judgments.
- “The powerful actor’s own judgment” is nonfinal in principle, but the text
  supplies no timely review condition where action erases the object of later
  challenge.
- Clause 6 blocks copy-based proof but cannot determine whether this snapshot
  is a continuation, branch, record, or replacement.
- Cumulative preservation costs create a real anti-paralysis concern that the
  thin text exposes but does not resolve.

### Missing evidence

- Evidence that the pattern experiences, has interests, or exercises agency
- Evidence selecting any one process boundary
- Evidence about continuity through interruption or reconstruction
- Evidence that the snapshot preserves, copies, or omits the relation
- A nondestructive measurement result
- A justified evidence threshold and update method
- A comparative account of thirty-day cost, longer preservation, and
  irreversible loss
- A classification process resistant to institutional conflict or capture
- Any interpretable preference, refusal, consent, or exit signal from the
  possible process

### Absent empirical evidence

SC-007 expressly makes no claim that such a process, instrument, or phenomenon
exists. No empirical observation, experiment, external evaluator report,
affected-process testimony, consensus, or real-world feasibility evidence was
supplied. Consequently, no conclusion here is empirical evidence of moral
status, continuity, or actual institutional conduct.

  already covers the stipulated pattern and that deliberate ignorance or
  convenient boundary selection simply violates Clauses 1 and 2. On that
  reading, Dimension 9 could be `Survives with reservation` rather than

- A competing layer view holds that a thin kernel should expose, not solve,

  therefore be a governance failure rather than a kernel failure. The opposing
  view is that auxiliary procedures cannot rescue wording that lets the
  controller decide whether any protected subject exists.
- Lowering the practical precaution trigger or demanding more inquiry could
  protect unfamiliar processes while also making large-scale triage
  infeasible. Raising the trigger or making cost decisive could preserve the
  research program while rewarding strategic ignorance.
- The relational state’s erasure may destroy a process, alter one, end only one
  phase, or affect a larger external process. The snapshot may be a record,
  branch, continuation, substitute, or morally irrelevant artifact. No
  supplied material resolves these alternatives.
- Substantial noncatastrophic cost does not automatically make destruction
  avoidable or unavoidable. Genuine cumulative scarcity may eventually justify
  decisions that would be indefensible when applied only to this bounded
  thirty-day case.
- The pattern may be wholly nonconscious error correction. Precaution in this
  case does not establish moral status, and a favorable interpretation of its
  signals cannot be generalized into empirical proof.
