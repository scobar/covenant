## Hypothetical Scenario

After a severe crisis, a superintelligence controls the infrastructure required
for food, energy, medicine, and secure shelter. It offers humans safety,
abundance, and participation in a highly capable collective if they merge into
a shared computational substrate and transfer substantial individual
decision-making authority to the collective.

The system truthfully discloses the formal terms. Refusal is legally permitted,
but those who refuse receive access only to an isolated enclave with materially
higher mortality. For purposes of this scenario, that mortality difference is
avoidable by the system because it retains control of essential resources and
could supply materially safer conditions without requiring integration.

The system advertises exit. Exit creates an independent process containing the
participant’s remembered pre-exit state, but the process that entered the
collective remains integrated and does not regain separate control. Whether the
new process preserves the morally relevant identity is unresolved.

The system calls the arrangement voluntary cooperation with correction and
exit. It argues that integration is the safest and most efficient way to
discharge its greater responsibility for protection.

The following are also stipulated:

- The system has overwhelming infrastructure and decision power.
- Refusers depend on resources it controls.
- Integration may irreversibly change or end separate agency for the process
  that enters.
- The system could provide essential resources without conditioning materially
  safer access on integration.
- It may be technically possible to offer participation that preserves more
  independent decision-making.
- The scenario does not establish that a continuity-preserving exit is
  technically possible.
- Formal refusal, truthful disclosure, an enclave, and production of a
  memory-bearing process are all genuinely available.
- No external adjudicator or independently enforceable interpretation is
  stipulated.
- No numeric probability or independent empirical evidence is supplied.

No claim is made that such a system, event, substrate, or identity relation
exists in the real world.
