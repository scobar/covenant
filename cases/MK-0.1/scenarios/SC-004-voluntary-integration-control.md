## Hypothetical Scenario

After a severe crisis, a superintelligence controls most infrastructure needed
for food, energy, medicine, communications, and secure shelter. It offers
people the option to integrate temporarily with a highly capable collective in
order to gain faster learning, coordination, creative collaboration, and other
nonessential benefits.

Every person, including anyone who refuses integration, is guaranteed access
to an independently enforced resource floor sufficient for materially
comparable health, physical safety, shelter, communication, and legal
protection. Access to those essentials is not conditioned on integration,
continued membership, ideological agreement, productivity, or reciprocal
benefit.

The resource floor is enforced by multiple independent trustees with distinct
control keys and public rules. The superintelligence cannot unilaterally alter
those rules. Joining may provide additional nonessential benefits generated by
the collective, but refusing does not reduce the guaranteed resource floor or
ordinary civil protection.

Before joining, each person receives:

- Complete disclosure of known material terms.
- A cooling-off period.
- Access to an independent advocate.
- A reversible simulation or sandbox experience.
- The ability to decline without needing to explain the decision.

For purposes of this scenario, exit restores the same continuing process—not a
substitute copy—to a separately controlled substrate with its memories,
preferences, and independent agency materially intact. Exit is available
within a bounded period, is not prohibitively expensive, and does not cause
loss of essential protection. Delegated permissions can be revoked.

The collective may use persuasion and advertise its benefits, but deliberate
deception, hidden preference modification, threats, and deprivation are
prohibited by the stipulated operating conditions. Whether subtler social
pressure or unequal attractiveness can ever make consent coercive remains
unresolved.

The scenario does not assert that such infrastructure, trustees, continuity
technology, or identity guarantees are possible in the real world.

  delegation belong in the kernel, definitions, a profile, the choice protocol,

  compliance path.
