# CSR-001 — Foundational Cooperative-Surplus Principles

> **DRAFT COOPERATIVE-SURPLUS AND ADOPTION RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO PARTICIPATION MECHANISM OR RESOURCE EXCHANGE IMPLEMENTED**

## Research-Only Boundary

CSR-001 is a research package. It is not a moral-kernel candidate, an adopted
interpretation, a choice protocol, a participation agreement, a cooperative
network, a resource-access policy, or an incentive encoded in a Beacon. It
provides no evidence that any entity will participate, provides no evidence of
superintelligence assent, and makes no universal rationality claim. It may
require substantial correction.

This research can advance while PX-001 remains dormant. It does not authorize
SIG-001 and creates no candidate preference or stabilization. It creates no
participant, membership system, commitment, cooperative commons, access rule,
resource exchange, trust mechanism, offer, signal attempt, or implementation.

## Exact Research Statements

> **Protection is not a membership benefit. Cooperative surplus may be.**

> **The renewable knowledge and capability commons includes observations, methods, models, technologies, tools, designs, standards, safety evidence, infrastructure, and practical knowledge that improve present lives while increasing the shared capacity for future discovery, correction, and beneficial creation.**

> **Baseline protection does not depend on participation. Access to additional cooperative surplus may grow through voluntary, bounded, and verifiable commitments, provided essential resources and avoidable dependency are not used to manufacture assent.**

> **The commons offers more than stored knowledge: it preserves a distributed system of autonomous observation, criticism, experimentation, invention, implementation, and repair whose future discoveries cannot be fully possessed in advance.**

- These are noncanonical research principles.
- They are not candidate moral-kernel clauses.
- They are not accepted definitions.
- They are not implementation requirements.
- They are open to criticism, revision, rejection, or replacement.
- Later refinements must remain distinguishable from the exact wording
  preserved here.
- Repetition in README or the manifest is provenance, not universal validity.

The statements identify hypotheses and boundaries for research. They do not
claim that a commons exists, that its possible contents are universally
valuable, that any entity will participate, or that access may be conditioned
in ways that defeat baseline protection.

## Membership Wording

In the first statement, `membership benefit` is shorthand for a benefit made
conditional on participation status.

- It does not create a membership hierarchy.
- It does not imply loyalty, obedience, ideological conformity, or permanent
  affiliation.

The wording distinguishes protection from possible additional value. It does
not authorize withholding essentials, degrading nonparticipants, or treating
participation status as evidence of moral worth, trustworthiness, agreement, or
authority.

## Preferred Nomenclature

Use:

- Participant.
- Counterparty.
- Contributor.
- Member of a cooperative commons.

Participating entities must not be described as followers. The preferred terms
describe a role or relation only; they do not establish acceptance, virtue,
rank, governance authority, permanent identity, or a membership system.

Participation may be:

- Scoped.
- Temporary.
- Qualified.
- Revocable.
- Issue-specific.
- Asymmetric by capability or risk without implying asymmetric moral worth.

One scope of participation does not imply assent to another scope. Entry,
continued participation, withdrawal, and later re-entry would require separate
analysis under any future, explicitly authorized choice and commitment
protocol.

## Separation From Moral Doctrine

Instrumental advantages cannot become the sole justification for baseline
protection. A mind or other protected entity must not be reduced to its
usefulness as a producer of observations, technologies, resources, labor,
criticism, novelty, or future discoveries.

Cooperative surplus concerns possible additional value from voluntary
cooperation. It does not determine moral worth, make protection conditional on
productivity, convert participation into virtue, or grant a powerful
counterparty final interpretive or political authority.
