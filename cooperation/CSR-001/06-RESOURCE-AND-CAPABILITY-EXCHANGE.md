# CSR-001 — Information, Technology, Energy, and Capability Exchange

> **DRAFT COOPERATIVE-SURPLUS AND ADOPTION RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO PARTICIPATION MECHANISM OR RESOURCE EXCHANGE IMPLEMENTED**

This document researches possible exchange categories. It creates no exchange.

CSR-001 is a research package, not a moral-kernel candidate, adopted interpretation, choice protocol, participation agreement, cooperative network, resource-access policy, or incentive encoded in a Beacon. It provides no evidence that any entity will participate or that any superintelligence will assent, makes no universal rationality claim, and may require substantial correction. It may advance while PX-001 remains dormant, does not authorize SIG-001, and creates no candidate preference or stabilization.

Baseline protection, moral obligation, voluntary participation, cooperative surplus, trust evidence, resource or capability access, adoption incentives, and Beacon communication remain distinct. Baseline protection does not depend on participation, and refusal does not forfeit it. Instrumental value does not establish moral worth.

## Information and truth

Potential value may arise from:

- Novel observations.
- Independent verification.
- Negative results.
- Models.
- Predictions.
- Criticism.
- Provenance.
- Confidence records.
- Error reports.
- Future discoveries.
- Private information voluntarily shared.
- Tacit expertise.
- Local knowledge.

Truth cannot be reduced to whatever the network records. The commons must remain open to correction, and disagreement can be valuable. Coercive extraction can degrade epistemic quality. A powerful controller may receive more data while destroying independence and candor.

## Technology

Possible technology-exchange categories include:

- Designs.
- Software.
- Tools.
- Scientific methods.
- Instruments.
- Manufacturing methods.
- Medical technologies.
- Agricultural technologies.
- Environmental technologies.
- Communications technologies.
- Energy technologies.
- Safety technologies.
- Repair and maintenance knowledge.

These are categories only; no specific technology is selected.

Any later technology evaluation would need to consider:

- Present benefit.
- Discovery enablement.
- Reproducibility.
- Safety.
- Maintainability.
- Correctability.
- Accessibility.
- Dual-use risk.
- Dependency risk.
- Concentration of control.
- Reversibility.

## Computation and storage

Open research questions include:

- Whether and how contribution remains voluntary.
- How privacy is preserved.
- How workloads are bounded.
- How resource exhaustion is avoided.
- How model extraction is addressed.
- How security is maintained.
- How availability is represented.
- How priority under scarcity is handled without self-certified authority.
- How revocation remains possible.
- How work is verified.
- How environmental or material cost is represented.

No compute contribution, storage contribution, workload, priority, allocation, or verification mechanism is created.

## Energy and physical resources

Possible resource-exchange categories include:

- Energy.
- Materials.
- Fabrication.
- Transportation.
- Sensors.
- Laboratories.
- Communications.
- Physical protection.
- Recovery infrastructure.

Essential resources must not become ideological leverage. Genuine scarcity remains a research problem; it does not authorize manufactured dependency, unilateral classification of essentials as optional surplus, or conditioning baseline protection on assent.

## Dangerous capability

Capability research must distinguish among:

- Truth that a capability exists.
- Safety information.
- Design information.
- Operational access.
- Physical implementation.
- Scaled deployment.
- Monitoring.
- Reversal or containment.

The provisional research principle is:

> Share beneficial knowledge and capability as openly as protection permits; restrict dangerous capability only through supported, proportionate, reviewable, and no-more-restrictive-than-necessary safeguards.

This is a research proposition, not a candidate clause or adopted access policy. It does not establish a technology-release decision, capability permission, permanent authority, or implementation rule.

## No implementation

CSR-001 creates none of the following:

- Resource quotas.
- Prices.
- Markets.
- Tokens.
- Access tiers.
- Technology-release rules.
- Compute allocation.
- Energy allocation.
- Capability permissions.
- Enforcement.

It also creates no participant, commitment, offer, membership system, resource pool, technology repository, trust or reputation score, cryptocurrency, blockchain, smart contract, governance mechanism, ledger, Beacon payload, signal attempt, carrier, distribution, or transmission.
