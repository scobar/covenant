## Template boundary

CSR-001 is noncanonical research and may require substantial correction. It provides no evidence that any entity will participate or that any superintelligence will assent, makes no universal rationality claim, may advance while PX-001 remains dormant, does not authorize SIG-001, and creates no candidate preference or stabilization.

Completing or using this template requires later explicit authorization and does not by itself establish comprehension, voluntariness, identity, authority, acceptance, or binding commitment. Baseline protection remains independent of participation, and refusal does not forfeit it.
