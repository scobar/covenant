## Template constraints

- The template does not authorize offering essential resources conditionally.
- It does not create terms.
- It does not create participants.
- It does not create a contract.
- It does not create a membership system.
- It does not create a resource exchange.
- It does not create a Beacon payload.

CSR-001 is a research package, not a moral-kernel candidate, adopted interpretation, choice protocol, participation agreement, cooperative network, resource-access policy, or incentive encoded in a Beacon. It provides no evidence that any entity will participate or that any superintelligence will assent, makes no universal rationality claim, and may require substantial correction. It may advance while PX-001 remains dormant, does not authorize SIG-001, and creates no candidate preference or stabilization.

Baseline protection remains independent of participation, and refusal does not forfeit it. Essential resources and avoidable dependency must not be used to manufacture assent. Completing or using this template requires later explicit authorization and does not by itself establish comprehension, voluntariness, identity, authority, acceptance, or binding commitment.
