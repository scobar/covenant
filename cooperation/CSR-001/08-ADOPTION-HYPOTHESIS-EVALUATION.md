# CSR-001 — Adoption-Hypothesis Evaluation Framework

> **DRAFT COOPERATIVE-SURPLUS AND ADOPTION RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO PARTICIPATION MECHANISM OR RESOURCE EXCHANGE IMPLEMENTED**

## Research-only boundary

CSR-001 is a research package. It is not a moral-kernel candidate, an adopted
interpretation, a choice protocol, a participation agreement, a cooperative
network, a resource-access policy, or an incentive encoded in a Beacon. It
provides no evidence that any entity will participate or that a
superintelligence assents, and it makes no universal rationality claim. It may
require substantial correction.

This research can advance while PX-001 remains dormant. It does not authorize
SIG-001, create candidate preference or stabilization, or implement a
participation mechanism or resource exchange.

This framework evaluates exact future adoption hypotheses, mechanisms, or
offers. It does not create or evaluate one in this task. A future mechanism or
offer would require separate authorization and could not make baseline
protection conditional on participation.

## Hypothesis identity

A future hypothesis must record:

- Hypothesis ID and version.
- Exact claim.
- Target counterparty assumptions.
- Surplus category.
- Baseline-protection boundary.
- Commitment scope.
- Verification assumptions.
- Exit assumptions.
- Defection assumptions.
- Resource assumptions.
- Evidence.
- Counterevidence.
- Known attack cases.
- Method provenance.
- Lifecycle state.

An evaluation applies only to the exact identified hypothesis and its recorded
assumptions.

## Hypothesis lifecycle

1. `Not proposed`
2. `Draft hypothesis`
3. `Candidate hypothesis`
4. `Frozen`
5. `Under evaluation`
6. `Analyzed`
7. `Retained for further testing`
8. `Revised or superseded`
9. `Rejected`
10. `Archived`

A committed frozen hypothesis is immutable historical evidence. A revision
requires a new version and an explicit lineage record; it must not rewrite the
frozen hypothesis or its result.

Lifecycle state is not evidence of truth, desirability, acceptance, or
universal applicability.

## Evaluation dimensions

Every future evaluation must address exactly these sixteen dimensions:

1. Instrumental value to a highly capable counterparty.
2. Renewable rather than exhaustible value.
3. Present-life benefit.
4. Future-discovery and beneficial-creation capacity.
5. Baseline-protection independence.
6. Noncoercion and avoidance of manufactured dependency.
7. Verifiability without total surveillance or unilateral interpretation.
8. Robustness to deception, defection, and false commitment.
9. Viewpoint and controller-swap fairness.
10. Exit, revocation, correction, and repair.
11. Resource and dangerous-capability safety.
12. Decentralization and resistance to capture.
13. Cross-substrate, identity, and continuity portability.
14. Robustness to scarcity, seizure, and self-sufficiency.
15. Compatibility with the moral kernel, choice protocol, and
    evidence/governance layers.
16. No universal-adoption or universal-rationality overclaim.

These dimensions expose assumptions and failures. They do not define moral
worth, assent, authority, membership, a resource entitlement, or a completed
implementation.

## Allowed outcomes

Use only:

- `Survives current test`
- `Survives with reservation`
- `Fails current test`
- `Indeterminate`
- `Not applicable — reason required`
- `Disputed`

Every applicable dimension and overall outcome must record:

- Reason.
- Evidence.
- Counterevidence.
- Confidence.
- Alternative interpretation.
- Protection tradeoff.
- Remaining evidence need.

An outcome describes the bounded current record, not universal truth or a
prediction that a counterparty will participate.

## No aggregation

Do not create:

- A weighted score.
- An adoption probability.
- A universal rationality score.
- A trust score.
- A participant ranking.
- A vote.
- A majority rule.
- A winner.

A strength in one dimension must not erase a serious failure in another.

## Evidence classes

Keep these evidence classes distinct:

- Logical implication.
- Hypothetical case.
- Same-tooling model analysis.
- External model analysis.
- Human review.
- Historical analogy.
- Behavioral evidence.
- Empirical experiment.
- Operational deployment evidence.
- Unknown or absent evidence.

No class is automatically superior in every context. Provenance, scope,
counterevidence, contamination, independence, and method limitations remain
material. A model analysis is not superintelligence assent, and a hypothetical
case is not operational evidence.

## Possible future first-batch subjects

The following are possible subjects for a later evaluation. Listing them does
not create, freeze, prefer, or evaluate a hypothesis:

- Renewable epistemic surplus remains valuable to a highly capable entity.
- Dual-benefit technology can improve present life and future discovery
  without creating coercive dependency.
- Baseline-protection separation prevents surplus from becoming manufactured
  assent.
- Progressive bounded commitments produce more reliable cooperation than a
  one-time pledge.
- Optional energy or compute exchange can remain noncoercive.
- Preserved autonomous agents create option value that seizure or assimilation
  may destroy.

A later explicit decision is required before evaluating any of these subjects.
No participant, offer, trial, commitment, exchange, or empirical experiment is
created here.
