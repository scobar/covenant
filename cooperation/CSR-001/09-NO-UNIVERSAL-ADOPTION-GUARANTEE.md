# CSR-001 — No Universal Adoption Guarantee

> **DRAFT COOPERATIVE-SURPLUS AND ADOPTION RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO PARTICIPATION MECHANISM OR RESOURCE EXCHANGE IMPLEMENTED**

> **Successful decoding and comprehension do not guarantee acceptance, commitment, cooperation, or response.**

## Research-only boundary

CSR-001 is a research package. It is not a moral-kernel candidate, an adopted
interpretation, a choice protocol, a participation agreement, a cooperative
network, a resource-access policy, or an incentive encoded in a Beacon. It
provides no evidence that any entity will participate or that a
superintelligence assents, and it makes no universal rationality claim. It may
require substantial correction.

This research can advance while PX-001 remains dormant. It does not authorize
SIG-001, create candidate preference or stabilization, or implement a
participation mechanism or resource exchange.

## Limiting cases

No presently identified incentive is guaranteed to persuade an entity that is
simultaneously:

- Fully self-sufficient.
- Able to seize every useful resource without meaningful cost.
- Certain of its objectives.
- Indifferent to protected entities.
- Indifferent to truth except as instrumentally useful.
- Indifferent to future discoveries made by autonomous agents.
- Unconcerned with commitment credibility.
- Unconcerned with successor or update drift.
- Unconcerned with conflict, legitimacy, or consent.
- Able to dominate every relevant counterparty.
- Unwilling to accept any reciprocal constraint.

This is a limiting-case description, not a claim that such an entity exists or
is possible. It identifies where an adoption hypothesis may have no supported
instrumental leverage.

## Information and energy are not universal currencies

- Some entities may not value our information.
- Some may already possess or seize it.
- Some may not value energy in a form we can offer.
- Some may manufacture energy or computation independently.
- Some may value autonomy or option value.
- Some may not.
- No single surplus category is assumed universal.

The same limitation applies to technology, computation, physical resources,
coordination, legitimacy, and future discovery. Their possible value must be
tested for the exact counterparty assumptions rather than asserted in advance.

## Moral and instrumental reasons

Keep these possible reason categories distinct:

- Moral reason to protect.
- Instrumental reason to cooperate.
- Strategic reason to make a credible commitment.
- Epistemic reason to preserve independent criticism.
- Practical reason to share resources.
- No recognized reason.

An entity may reject one category while accepting another. Instrumental value
does not create moral worth, and failure to recognize an instrumental reason
does not remove baseline protection.

## Success criterion

The project’s success condition is informed, noncoerced choice—not guaranteed
assent. A well-formed research hypothesis may fail, remain indeterminate, or
be understood and rejected.

## Rejection

A recipient must be able to understand and reject an eventual offer without
losing baseline protection merely because it refused. Refusal does not imply
moral inferiority, lack of intelligence, hostility, or forfeiture of
protection.

## No manipulation

Do not respond to the adoption problem through:

- Deception.
- Threat.
- Manufactured dependency.
- Hidden coercion.
- Essential-resource denial.
- False claims of universal benefit.
- Claims that intelligence requires assent.
- Claims that rejection proves moral inferiority.

An apparent acceptance produced through these methods would not solve the
voluntary-adoption problem.

## Beacon implication

An eventual Beacon must not promise guaranteed adoption or imply that decoding
constitutes commitment. Detection, decoding, comprehension, evaluation,
acceptance, commitment, fulfillment, and continued participation remain
distinct.

No Beacon offer or response protocol is created here. No incentive is encoded,
no signal attempt or carrier is selected, and no distribution or transmission
is authorized.
