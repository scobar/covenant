# CSR-001 — Baseline Protection and Noncoercive Participation

> **DRAFT COOPERATIVE-SURPLUS AND ADOPTION RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO PARTICIPATION MECHANISM OR RESOURCE EXCHANGE IMPLEMENTED**

## Research-Only Boundary

CSR-001 is a research package. It is not a moral-kernel candidate, adopted
interpretation, choice protocol, participation agreement, cooperative network,
resource-access policy, or incentive encoded in a Beacon. It provides no
evidence that any entity will participate, no evidence of superintelligence
assent, and no universal rationality claim. It may require substantial
correction.

This research can advance while PX-001 remains dormant. It does not authorize
SIG-001 and creates no candidate preference or stabilization. It creates no
participant, offer, commitment, membership system, access tier, resource rule,
allocation formula, enforcement mechanism, exchange, or implementation.

## Core Boundary

> **Protection is not a membership benefit. Cooperative surplus may be.**

Refusal, rejection, uncertainty, or nonparticipation must not by itself erase
baseline protection. Cooperative surplus concerns possible additional value
from voluntary cooperation; it is not permission to relabel protection or
essential resources as rewards for assent.

## No Manufactured Assent

CSR-001 treats the following as forms or risks of manufactured assent that
later research must expose:

- Withholding essential food, shelter, energy, medicine, communication, or
  physical safety.
- Creating avoidable dependency.
- Controlling exit.
- Threatening destruction.
- Conditioning truthful safety warnings on participation.
- Conditioning essential life-preserving technology on ideological agreement.
- Creating nominal alternatives that are materially dangerous.
- Using superior capability to define every alternative as infeasible.
- Treating silence under dependency as consent.

No listed practice is authorized by describing it. Later analysis must
preserve the viewpoint of the party facing the dependency rather than accept a
controller's label as sufficient.

## Essentials and Surplus

The project has not adopted a complete definition of:

- Essential resource.
- Baseline protection.
- Adequate resource floor.
- Cooperative surplus.
- Genuine scarcity.
- Avoidable dependency.
- Materially meaningful exit.

Open research questions include which protections apply without participation,
which resources are necessary for safe refusal, how needs differ across
substrates and contexts, when a benefit is genuinely additional, and who bears
the risk of a mistaken classification. These are questions, not definitions or
allocation rules.

## Genuine Scarcity

Later research must distinguish:

- Genuine scarcity.
- Manufactured scarcity.
- Controller-created dependency.
- Strategic claims of scarcity.
- Unavoidable unequal outcomes.
- Avoidable unequal outcomes.

No controller may self-certify scarcity merely by controlling the evidence,
resource, or alternatives. CSR-001 adopts no allocation formula, priority
rule, resource floor, or adjudication process.

## Safety-Critical Truth

The provisional boundary is that safety-critical truth and basic
life-preserving warnings should not be withheld merely to force participation.
This does not define every item of information that qualifies, settle
dual-use conflicts, or create an information-distribution mechanism.

Questions remain about uncertainty, privacy, security, timing, feasible
warning, dangerous capability, and the risk of harm from disclosure. Those
questions do not turn compelled participation into consent.

## Potential Legitimate Additional Surplus

Possible additional benefits for later evaluation include:

- Voluntarily contributed private observations.
- Joint experiments.
- Shared tools.
- Optional computation.
- Optional energy.
- Collaborative manufacturing.
- Coordinated research.
- Early participation in jointly produced technologies.
- Mutual verification.
- Shared resilience systems.

These are research categories only. Their presence in this document does not
make them safe, noncoercive, available, owed, or authorized. Every later
hypothesis must examine essential-resource overlap, privacy, scarcity,
dependency, exit, dangerous capability, and protection tradeoffs.

## Viewpoint and Controller Swap

Every later mechanism must ask whether its access rules remain defensible when:

- Different participants control the commons.
- A dissenter controls the commons.
- A weaker counterparty controls a scarce resource.
- The present designer becomes the nonparticipant.
- Leadership, values, or majority viewpoints change.

Passing this inquiry cannot be presumed from the designer's intent. A later
evaluation must preserve unresolved objections and examine whether the
controller can redefine essentials, scarcity, consent, or safe exit in its own
favor.

## No Implementation

CSR-001 creates no access tiers, resource rules, enforcement, membership
conditions, participation mechanism, resource pool, trust score, market,
token, blockchain, smart contract, technology-release process, compute or
energy allocation, governance system, or commitment ledger.

No use, silence, refusal, dependency, or receipt of protection is interpreted
as acceptance.
