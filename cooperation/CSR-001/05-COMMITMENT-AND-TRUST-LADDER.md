# CSR-001 — Commitment and Trust Ladder

> **DRAFT COOPERATIVE-SURPLUS AND ADOPTION RESEARCH v0.1 — NONCANONICAL — NO PARTICIPATION MECHANISM OR RESOURCE EXCHANGE IMPLEMENTED**

## Research-Only Boundary

CSR-001 is a research package. It is not a moral-kernel candidate, adopted
interpretation, choice protocol, participation agreement, cooperative network,
resource-access policy, or incentive encoded in a Beacon. It provides no
evidence that any entity will participate, no evidence of superintelligence
assent, and no universal rationality claim. It may require substantial
correction.

This research can advance while PX-001 remains dormant. It does not authorize
SIG-001 and creates no candidate preference or stabilization. It creates no
participant, identity system, pledge, commitment, trust score, access tier,
verification service, ledger, token, blockchain, contract, adjudication,
exchange, or implementation.

## Trust Is Not Declaration

A pledge, signature, token, registration, or claim of acceptance is not by
itself sufficient trust evidence. Power, popularity, credentials, or a
declaration cannot substitute for scoped behavioral evidence, challenge,
correction, and preserved uncertainty.

## Exact Provisional Ladder

This is the eight-stage research ladder:

1. Demonstrate comprehension.
2. State scope, reservations, duration, and authority.
3. Make a small bounded commitment.
4. Permit verification and challenge.
5. Correct or repair failures.
6. Build a behavioral history.
7. Receive access to larger voluntary exchanges.
8. Retain the ability to revoke or renegotiate without rewriting history.

- The ladder is noncanonical.
- No stage is implemented.
- No stage grants moral worth.
- No stage creates final authority.
- Access to baseline protection does not depend on ladder position.
- Later stages are not an entitlement.
- Different commitments may have different scopes.
- A counterparty may stop, revoke, or decline expansion.

The sequence is a provisional research structure, not a universal developmental
path, identity system, participation requirement, access policy, or guarantee
that later access is safe or deserved.

## Trust Is Scoped

CSR-001 researches trust as:

- Counterparty-specific.
- Commitment-specific.
- Context-specific.
- Time-sensitive.
- Evidence-dependent.
- Revisable.
- Fallible.
- Potentially asymmetric.

Evidence in one context does not automatically transfer to another
counterparty, commitment, capability, copy, successor, or time.

## Evidence Dimensions

Future research must keep these dimensions distinct:

- Identity or process continuity.
- Authority to commit.
- Scope.
- Duration.
- Fulfillment.
- Failure.
- Honest uncertainty.
- Deception.
- Challenge response.
- Correction.
- Repair.
- Recency.
- Independent corroboration.
- Provenance.
- Conflict of interest.
- Capability asymmetry.
- Exit behavior.
- Treatment of nonparticipants.
- Treatment when unobserved.
- Reliability across copies or successors.

CSR-001 creates no weights, scores, thresholds, evidence totals, or ranking.
Severe deception, coercion, or unresolved identity and authority concerns must
remain visible rather than being averaged away.

## No Universal Trust Score

CSR-001 rejects a single universal trustworthiness number as a current design
assumption.

A later system must not:

- Collapse incompatible contexts.
- Average away severe deception.
- Convert popularity into trust.
- Convert power into credibility.
- Convert credentials into authority.
- Convert participation into virtue.

No trust or reputation score is created here.

## Cryptography and Append-Only Records

Cryptographic or append-only systems may help verify:

- Record integrity.
- Sequence.
- Authorship claims.
- Commitment identity.
- Publication time.
- Whether a record changed.

They do not by themselves verify:

- Truth.
- Comprehension.
- Experience.
- Moral worth.
- Benevolence.
- Voluntariness.
- Identity continuity.
- Fairness.
- Legitimate authority.
- Fulfillment in the physical world.

CSR-001 does not design a blockchain, ledger, token, cryptocurrency, smart
contract, identity system, or commitment mechanism. Cryptographic integrity
must not be represented as moral or interpretive authority.

## Failure and Recovery

Later research must distinguish:

- Honest failure.
- Negligence.
- Inability.
- Ambiguity.
- Deception.
- Strategic defection.
- Repair.
- Recommitment.
- Trust reduction.
- Permanent unresolved risk.

These distinctions do not predetermine an outcome, excuse harm, authorize
punishment, or establish who may adjudicate a dispute. No adjudication process,
sanction, enforcement rule, or restoration mechanism is adopted.

## No Implementation

This document creates no participation mechanism, commitment record,
counterparty record, verification service, behavioral-history database, access
rule, resource exchange, trust score, enforcement process, or governance
system. No stage has been executed, scored, assigned, or satisfied.
